How is Podcast Sponsor Evidence Report Scraper different from Apple Podcasts Scraper and Apple Podcasts Reviews Scraper?

Podcast Sponsor Evidence Report Scraper (taroyamada/podcast-sponsor-evidence-report-scraper): This Actor extracts podcast sponsor evidence from RSS feeds and show URLs you supply. First paid Input: 1 RSS + maxEpisodes 3, export off. Not a media-buyer shortlist pack. Not Apple charts/search. Not advertiser contact enrichment. Public RSS only. Apple Podcasts Scraper is for iTunes search, charts, episodes (input searchTerm; Apple/iTunes endpoints; Podcast metadata rows). Not RSS-only sponsor evidence. Apple Podcasts Reviews Scraper is for Public review RSS (input collection IDs; Customer Reviews RSS; Review rows). Not sponsor-signal reports. RSS & Atom Feed Extractor is for Known-publisher feed XML (input feed URLs; RSS/Atom XML; Feed item rows). Not sponsor-evidence extraction.

What input is required?

Live required fields: none listed (see live fields). Published exampleRunInput is shown below.

Field Type Default Notes
rssFeedUrls string[] ["https://feeds.npr.org/510289/podcast.xml"] RSS feed URLs. Public podcast RSS feed URLs to inspect.
showUrls string[] empty Show URLs. Public podcast show or publisher pages to inspect.
searchTerms string[] ["business podcast sponsorship research"] Apple Podcast search terms. Optional Apple iTunes Search API terms for feed discovery.
maxEpisodesPerFeed integer 3 Max episodes per feed. Maximum RSS episode items to inspect per feed. minimum=1 maximum=50
emitResearchExport boolean false Emit research export. Emit one sponsorship research export row when evidence rows are found.
emitUnchanged boolean false Emit unchanged rows. When false, previously seen evidence is suppressed.
dryRun boolean false Dry run. When true, emit sample rows locally without external live calls or billing.
