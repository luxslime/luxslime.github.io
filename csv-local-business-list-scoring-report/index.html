How is CSV Local Business List Scoring Report different from CSV Cleaner & Deduplication Tool and Google Maps Lead Enrichment?

CSV Local Business List Scoring Report (taroyamada/csv-local-business-list-scoring-report): This Actor turns pasted CSV business lists into CRM hygiene, local SEO gap, and prioritization reports without scraping Google Maps or extracting contacts. Does not scrape Google Maps. Does not extract emails/phones from the web. Not a CSV cleaner (use CSV Cleaner & Deduplication Tool). You paste the list. CSV Cleaner & Deduplication Tool is for Trim/dedupe/sort user CSV (input CSV text; User-supplied CSV; Cleaned CSV rows). Not local-SEO scoring reports. Google Maps Lead Enrichment is for Enrich existing leads (input business inputs; Enrichment APIs; Lead rows). Does not scrape Maps or ATS boards. Contact Page Audit is for Public contact-channel extract (input page URLs; Public contact pages; Channel rows). Not CSV list scoring.
