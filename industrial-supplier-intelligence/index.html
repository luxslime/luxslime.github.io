How is Industrial Supplier Intelligence different from Trade Show Exhibitor Intelligence and Google Maps Lead Enrichment?

Industrial Supplier Intelligence (taroyamada/industrial-supplier-intelligence): This Actor enriches user-provided industrial supplier websites into B2B profiles with categories, capabilities, locations, certifications, contact pages, and confidence. Keywords are local hints against supplied pages, not search-engine queries. No search-engine discovery. Not a trade-show booth scrape. Not authenticated portals. Public HTML only. Not a supplier-approval decision. Trade Show Exhibitor Intelligence is for Booth fields from supplied URLs (input exhibitor URLs; User-supplied show pages; Exhibitor rows). Not ATS job boards. Google Maps Lead Enrichment is for Enrich existing leads (input business inputs; Enrichment APIs; Lead rows). Does not scrape Maps or ATS boards. TED, SAM.gov & Grants Bid Alerts is for Open opportunities / tenders (input keywords / jurisdictions; SAM/TED/Grants notices; Opportunity rows). Not vendor-pursuit profiles, Form D notices, or post-award spend reports.

What input is required?

Live required fields: none listed (see live fields). Published exampleRunInput is shown below.

Field Type Default Notes
urls string[] empty Supplier URLs. Specific supplier website URLs to enrich. Use public home, about, capabilities, products, certifications, quote, RFQ, or contact pages. No search engine discovery is used. maxItems=100
domains string[] empty Supplier Domains. Bare supplier domains from ERP, vendor master, procurement research, sourcing spreadsheets, or account lists. Domains are normalized to https://domain/ and fetched directly. maxItems=100
keywords string[] empty Procurement Keywords / Capability Hints. Industrial terms used only for local relevance scoring and category/capability hints against supplied URLs/domains. They are not sent to search engines. maxItems=50
region string empty Buyer Region / Sourcing Market. Optional buyer region, country, or sourcing market used as a location and relevance hint. Example: United States, Mexico, Germany, Japan, or Southeast Asia.
maxResults integer 25 Max Supplier Profiles. Maximum normalized supplier sources to process and emit. Keep this small for quickstarts; increase for recurring procurement shortlist refreshes. minimum=1 maximum=100
dryRun boolean false Dry Run. Validate input and write the local output summary without sending dataset rows, triggering webhook delivery, or charging custom PPE events.
maxChargeUsd number 6 Max Charge USD. Hard safety cap for custom PPE supplier events in this run. Rows that would exceed the cap are emitted as no-charge limit_reached diagnostics. minimum=0 maximum=100
