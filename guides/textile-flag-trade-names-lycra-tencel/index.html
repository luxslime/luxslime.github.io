Flag textile trade names

Flag Lycra and Tencel on fibre fields — do not treat trade names as Annex I composition

Direct answer. Shopify product fields accept any fibre string you type, including trade names like Lycra or Tencel, and Admin does not flag them against the EU Annex I catalogue. FibreLedger Textile Labels flags trade names like Lycra or Tencel that are not lawful fibre names so you can replace them with Annex I names before the product page ships.

FibreLedger Textile Labels (Shopify App Store) validates every fibre against the official Annex I catalogue (EN, DE, FR), flags trade names like Lycra or Tencel that are not lawful fibre names, and supports French places of manufacture and environmental-impact fields. This page is the trade-name flagging slice — not the full Annex I composition overview, language-of-market, French places, environmental-impact fields, or EPR records. This page is not legal advice.

Trade-name flags are not the same job as writing the composition line

The composition overview explains why Annex I names belong on the label. This page is the scan-and-flag job: catch Lycra, Tencel, and similar trade names in fibre fields before they reach the storefront.

How to flag trade names like Lycra or Tencel

  1. Run validation on product fibre composition fields.
  2. Review flags for trade names that are not Annex I fibre names.
  3. Replace flagged trade names with the lawful Annex I names you intend to show.
  4. Confirm the live product page no longer shows the trade name on the composition line.
