EU Food Info Guard vs NutritionFactsKit: Annex II gap review vs FDA Nutrition Facts panel

Direct answer. Choose NutritionFactsKit when you want an FDA-style Nutrition Facts panel on product pages, built from each product’s metafields (the app creates those fields on install), with the official FDA layout, optional colour-coded %DV and plain-language tooltips, as pure theme Liquid with no JavaScript. Choose EU Food Info Guard when you want the 14 EU Annex II allergens detected from ingredient text, an allergen the ingredients mention but the declared list omits flagged, pre-purchase facts separated from delivery-only notes, a theme-block sync, and weekly completeness reviews with an audit trail. This page is not a ranking and not legal advice. Distance-sale allergen information still has to be available before purchase under Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011 Article 14; confirm the live text. NutritionFactsKit describes a US FDA panel, not an EU FIC panel.

EU Food Info Guard (Shopify App Store · Write a review) reviews allergen, ingredient, nutrition, net quantity, origin, operator, and storage gaps before food product display. NutritionFactsKit (Shopify App Store) shows an FDA-style Nutrition Facts panel from product metafields. Neither listing is a determination that a product page meets FIC or FDA rules. Re-open both before you decide.

What FIC actually requires on a distance-sale page

For prepacked food sold at a distance, Article 14 of Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011 requires mandatory food information — including allergen information under Articles 9 and 21 and Annex II — to be available before the purchase is concluded, and all mandatory particulars again at delivery. The Commission page on food allergies is a reading aid, not a substitute for the regulation. Neither app decides legal compliance. Confirm the live EUR-Lex text. An FDA Nutrition Facts panel is a different labelling regime from FIC.

How to choose

If you want the familiar US Nutrition Facts panel on product pages from metafields the app creates, with FDA layout and optional colour-coded %DV tooltips in pure Liquid, NutritionFactsKit fits that job. If you want Annex II allergens detected from ingredient text, mismatches against the declared list flagged, pre-purchase facts kept apart from delivery-only notes, a theme-block sync, and weekly completeness reviews with an audit trail, EU Food Info Guard fits that job. Display and gap review can sit side by side; this page does not say one listing replaces the other. Open NutritionFactsKit and EU Food Info Guard and re-read the live copy.
