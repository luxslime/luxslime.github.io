Recycled-content percent

A bare “recycled” badge is not a recycled-content percent — specify the share on the same product page

Green Claims Guard (Shopify App Store) scans green marketing wording in your catalog and content, flags green-claim wording and names the exact provision it cites, and exports dated evidence records for review. It does not decide whether a claim is lawful. This page is not legal advice.

Generic “recycled” vs a percent you can show on the same page

Unspecialised green wording on the product page — including bare “recycled” style claims without a clear share or scope — sits next to the generic environmental-claim job covered in EU generic green claims ban. A recycled-content percent on the same medium (for example “contains 30% post-consumer recycled plastic”) is a different, specified statement: the number and what it covers must appear where the shopper sees the claim. Confirm Directive (EU) 2024/825 and your counsel before you publish; this page does not invent a safe slogan.
