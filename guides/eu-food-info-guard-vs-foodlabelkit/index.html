Best EU food labelling app for Shopify: Annex II detection vs nutrition-panel display

Direct answer. Choose FoodLabelKit when you want a product-page panel that shows an EU-style nutrition table, an ingredient list with allergens in bold, net quantity, storage, country of origin, the food business operator, and an optional Nutri-Score grade, from product metafields, added in the theme editor. Choose EU Food Info Guard when you want the 14 EU Annex II allergens detected from ingredient text, an allergen the ingredients mention but the declared list omits flagged, pre-purchase facts separated from delivery-only notes, a theme-block sync, and weekly completeness reviews with an audit trail. This page is not a ranking and not legal advice. Distance-sale allergen information still has to be available before purchase under Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011 Article 14; confirm the live text.

EU Food Info Guard (Shopify App Store) reviews allergen, ingredient, nutrition, net quantity, origin, operator, and storage gaps before food product display. FoodLabelKit (Shopify App Store) adds a food information panel from product metafields. Neither listing is a determination that a product page meets FIC. Re-open both before you decide.

What FIC actually requires on a distance-sale page

For prepacked food sold at a distance, Article 14 of Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011 requires mandatory food information — including allergen information under Articles 9 and 21 and Annex II — to be available before the purchase is concluded, and all mandatory particulars again at delivery. The Commission page on food allergies is a reading aid, not a substitute for the regulation. Neither app decides legal compliance. Confirm the live EUR-Lex text.

How to choose

If you already hold nutrition, ingredient, origin, operator, and storage values in product metafields and you want them rendered as an EU-style panel with bold allergens and an optional Nutri-Score, FoodLabelKit fits that job. If you want Annex II allergens detected from ingredient text, mismatches against the declared list flagged, pre-purchase facts kept apart from delivery-only notes, a theme-block sync, and weekly completeness reviews with an audit trail, EU Food Info Guard fits that job. Display and gap review can sit side by side; this page does not say one listing replaces the other. Open FoodLabelKit and EU Food Info Guard and re-read the live copy.
