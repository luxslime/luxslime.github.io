How to export Agentic Catalog CSV evidence on Shopify

Direct answer. A readiness score alone is not a per-product fix list you can hand to a developer. To export CSV evidence per product for AI-readiness fixes, install Agentic Catalog by momiji (App Store: agentic-catalog-readiness), run a scan, then export on the plan that includes CSV. Listing: Free — 5 scans/mo (llms.txt + robots + JSON-LD); Pro — $9.99/mo (or $99/year) scheduled scans + alerts; $24.99/mo — Beyond Pro: CSV evidence per product. The app does not replace Shopify Catalog syndication to agentic channels.

Why merchants ask this

After the first ChatGPT-readiness scan, ops teams ask for a spreadsheet of which products fail JSON-LD or Open Graph checks. Search interest in “Shopify ChatGPT shopping CSV”, “AI catalog readiness export”, and “product JSON-LD audit” maps to that evidence job. This how-to is the CSV export path — not the first readiness check and not the schedule/alerts sibling.

Steps — export per-product CSV evidence

  1. Install Agentic Catalog from the Shopify App Store (handle: agentic-catalog-readiness).
  2. Run a readiness scan against the live store (llms.txt, AI crawler robots, Product JSON-LD / Open Graph).
  3. On the $24.99/mo plan, export CSV evidence per product for the fix list.
  4. Re-scan after fixes. On Pro, schedule daily or weekly scans with email alerts when the score changes.
