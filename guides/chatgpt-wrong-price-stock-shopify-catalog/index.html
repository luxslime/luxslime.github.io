Agentic Catalog ・ ChatGPT ・ Catalog mapping

ChatGPT shows the wrong price or stock for my Shopify product

Direct answer. When ChatGPT states a price or availability that does not match your storefront, treat Catalog syndication and open-web crawl as separate sources. Shopify Help says Catalog continuously updates inventory and prices for agentic channels, and that custom titles/descriptions should be fixed on the Channel mapping page when data lives in metafields or metaobjects — mapping changes are processed with a delay. Community threads (for example LLM “out of stock” answers while the PDP is in stock) point merchants to Sales channels → Agentic and Catalog Mapping, not only theme code. Agentic Catalog by momiji does not edit Catalog Mapping; it audits what live product pages emit (Product JSON-LD and Open Graph), plus llms.txt and AI crawler robots rules, so you can see crawl-side mismatches after you correct admin data. Why merchants ask this

Merchant English includes “ChatGPT wrong price,” “LLM thinks my product is out of stock,” and “Catalog is integrated but the answer is still wrong.” Shopify Community and public Reddit threads (checked 2026-10-30) describe stale stock answers that robots.txt cannot fix because Catalog, not the theme HTML alone, feeds agentic channels.

Checklist (Catalog first, then crawl)

  1. Admin product truth. Confirm variant price and inventory in Shopify Admin match what you expect for the market ChatGPT is answering for.
  2. Catalog Mapping. If title, description, or category come from metafields/metaobjects, open Sales channels → Agentic → Review product data mapping (Shopify Help). Preview the product; change one source; wait for Catalog preview before re-testing ChatGPT.
  3. Compare-at vs price. Help notes you can choose whether Catalog uses price or compare-at as the list price for sale presentation.
  4. Propagation delay. Shopify documents mapping delay; do not treat an immediate ChatGPT retry as proof the mapping failed.
  5. Live page structured data. Compare Offer price / availability in Product JSON-LD on the live PDP with Admin. Agentic Catalog audits JSON-LD and Open Graph completeness on live URLs and can re-scan after fixes.

What Agentic Catalog can and cannot do here

  • Can: Flag incomplete or inconsistent Product JSON-LD / Open Graph on live pages; check llms.txt and AI crawler robots; score policy/shipping/tax/domain/product data; schedule scans; export CSV evidence on the higher plan.
  • Cannot: Rewrite Shopify Catalog Mapping, force ChatGPT to refresh a card, or guarantee that a corrected Admin field appears in the next ChatGPT answer.
