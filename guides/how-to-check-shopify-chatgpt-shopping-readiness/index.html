How to check if your Shopify store is ready for ChatGPT shopping

Direct answer. Before you treat ChatGPT shopping as a growth channel, confirm agent-facing basics on your live store: /llms.txt (and related agent discovery), robots rules for AI crawlers such as GPTBot, and Product JSON-LD / Open Graph completeness on product pages. Agentic Catalog by momiji (App Store: agentic-catalog-readiness) runs readiness scans for those checks, scores policy/shipping/tax/domain/product data together, can schedule daily or weekly scans with email alerts on score changes, and exports CSV evidence per product on a higher plan. Shopify Catalog syndication to agentic channels is separate from these discovery files — the app does not replace Catalog. Listing: Free (5 scans/mo: llms.txt + robots + JSON-LD); Pro $9.99/mo scheduled scans + alerts; $24.99/mo CSV evidence.

Why merchants ask this

Shopify Community thread “Getting traffic from ChatGPT & AI agents, where do I even start?” (checked 2026-10-30) shows merchants looking for a concrete first check — not another generic SEO checklist. Shopify Help for agentic storefronts and Catalog explains that eligible product data can syndicate through Shopify Catalog, while /agents.md, /llms.txt, and /llms-full.txt are discovery files that do not replace Catalog. This page is the readiness-scan how-to for those checks.

What to verify first

  1. Agent discovery URLs — Confirm /llms.txt (and typically /agents.md) respond on your storefront.
  2. AI crawler robots rules — Check whether GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot are allowed or blocked the way you intend.
  3. Product page structured data — Product JSON-LD and Open Graph fields complete on live product URLs.
  4. Store readiness signals — Policy, shipping, tax, domain, and product data contributing to one readiness score.
  5. Catalog / Agentic channel — Separately, review Sales channels → Agentic / Catalog eligibility in Shopify admin (Shopify Help). Turning off Catalog access does not stop open-web crawling.

Run an Agentic Catalog scan

Install Agentic Catalog, run a readiness scan against the live store, fix the highest-impact gaps the score highlights, then re-scan. On Pro, schedule daily or weekly scans with email alerts when the score changes. On the higher paid plan, export CSV evidence per product for the fix list.
