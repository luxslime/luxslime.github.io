Article 14 deadlines

Article 14 clocks are not the same job as an ENISA draft pack or an open-gap checklist

Direct answer. Shopify Admin has no Article 14 incident deadline clock on a product file — you can note dates in metafields or a spreadsheet, but nothing tracks CRA Article 14 clocks alongside role checklists and ENISA draft packs. CRALedger tracks Article 14 incident deadlines on the product duty file so the clock sits with the same records you already keep for roles and gaps.

CRALedger Duty Records (Shopify App Store) tracks product roles, checklists and open record gaps, Article 14 incident deadlines, encrypted ENISA SRP draft packs, vulnerability forwarding, and audit records. It does not file notifications on ENISA’s platform. This page is the Article 14 deadline slice — not open-gap checklists or ENISA packs. This page is not legal advice.

Clocks vs packs vs gaps

Open record gaps ask what is missing before an incident. Encrypted ENISA SRP draft packs prepare a filing. Article 14 deadlines answer when the clock runs after an incident is in play.

How to track Article 14 incident deadlines

  1. Open the product duty file in CRALedger.
  2. Record the Article 14 incident deadline fields the checklist expects.
  3. Keep role checklists and open gaps current so the clock is not running on an empty file.
  4. Use ENISA draft packs when you are preparing a filing — the deadline tracker does not submit to ENISA.
