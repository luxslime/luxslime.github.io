Address rules app comparison

AddressFence vs HBN: Address Rules — billing rules vs delivery-address policies

Direct answer. Choose HBN: Address Rules when you need delivery-address policies for PO boxes, military addresses, destinations, postal rules, and product targeting with customer messages. Choose AddressFence when you need billing-address rules and a street-number requirement that built-in checkout validation cannot apply, with a simulator and one-click rollback.

How to choose

If you need delivery-address policies across PO boxes, military addresses, destinations, postal rules, and product targeting with customer messages, HBN: Address Rules fits that job. If you need billing-address rules and a street-number requirement that built-in checkout validation cannot apply, with a simulator and one-click rollback, AddressFence fits that job.
