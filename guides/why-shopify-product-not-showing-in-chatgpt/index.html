Agentic Catalog ・ ChatGPT ・ Catalog eligibility

Why isn’t my Shopify product showing in ChatGPT?

Direct answer. Shopify Help for Shopify Catalog says eligible products are included automatically, and inclusion does not guarantee a product appears in a specific AI answer or rank. For ChatGPT specifically, Selling on ChatGPT requires selling to US customers, Catalog-eligible products, completed Terms / Privacy / Return policies, and agreement to Agentic Storefronts supplemental terms. First open Sales channels → Agentic and read Sources → Shopify Catalog: a product count means eligible; an Ineligible warning opens the store-level reason. Separately, open-web discovery still depends on crawler access and product-page structured data. Agentic Catalog by momiji (App Store: agentic-catalog-readiness) does not replace Catalog or change ChatGPT ranking; it scans /llms.txt, AI crawler robots rules, Product JSON-LD / Open Graph on live pages, and scores policy/shipping/tax/domain/product data together. Listing: Free (5 scans/mo: llms.txt + robots + JSON-LD); Pro $9.99/mo scheduled scans + alerts; $24.99/mo CSV evidence.

Why merchants ask this

Merchants search phrases such as “why isn’t my Shopify product showing in ChatGPT” and “ChatGPT shopping products not appearing.” Shopify Community threads on agentic shopping and LLM stock answers (checked 2026-10-30) show the same confusion: enrollment or Catalog access is treated as a guarantee of a product card. Reddit threads on Agentic enrollment vs appearance make the same split. This page answers the merchant checklist without promising ranking.

Three gates (in order)

  1. Store Catalog eligibility. In Sales channels → Agentic, open Sources → Shopify Catalog. Shopify Help says an Ineligible warning means the store has no products in Catalog for agentic channels that use it; click Ineligible to read Shopify’s reason list.
  2. Product-level Catalog requirements. Help says each product needs clear, complete data (title, images, price, availability, and related attributes) and must meet Catalog requirements. B2B-only / password-gated products are excluded from agentic storefronts when Shopify can identify them. ChatGPT also excludes products covered by OpenAI’s prohibited products list (Shopify Help links OpenAI Commerce policies).
  3. Channel ranking. Catalog puts eligible data in the pool. ChatGPT decides relevance and which merchants to show. There is no merchant “force show this SKU” switch.

Catalog path vs open-web crawl

Shopify Help states Catalog is the primary syndication path for agentic storefronts such as ChatGPT, and that blocking AI crawlers in robots.txt does not stop Catalog. Crawlers can still read the live storefront. Turning off Catalog access for ChatGPT (after disabling “Allow Shopify to manage for me”) filters Catalog data for that channel and can take up to 7 days; it does not stop crawl-based mentions. See agentic storefronts vs crawl.

What to check on the crawl / structured-data side

After Catalog eligibility looks healthy, confirm agent discovery URLs (/llms.txt, /agents.md), robots rules for GPTBot and other AI crawlers, and Product JSON-LD / Open Graph completeness on live product URLs. Run an Agentic Catalog readiness scan, fix the highest-impact gaps, and re-scan. On Pro, schedule daily or weekly scans with email alerts on score changes; on the higher paid plan, export CSV evidence per product.
