Policy / shipping / tax / domain readiness

AI catalog readiness is not only robots and JSON-LD — policy, shipping, tax, and domain sit in the same score

Direct answer. Yes: Agentic Catalog by momiji reviews policy, shipping, tax, domain, and product data in one readiness score, together with llms.txt and robots.txt rules for GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot and Product JSON-LD and Open Graph completeness on live product pages. Shopify Admin does not roll those into one AI shopping readiness score, because shipping, tax, and legal pages sit in separate Settings screens.

Agentic Catalog by momiji (Shopify App Store) checks llms.txt and robots.txt rules for GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot, audits Product JSON-LD and Open Graph completeness on live product pages, and reviews policy, shipping, tax, domain, and product data in one readiness score. This page is the policy / shipping / tax / domain slice of that score — not the crawler-file or JSON-LD siblings. The app does not compare Admin values per variant, market, or currency, and it cannot see what an AI assistant answered. This page is not legal advice.

Policy, shipping, tax, and domain are catalog readiness inputs — not theme polish

A store can pass a robots.txt glance and still fail agent readiness when legal policies, shipping truth, tax setup, or the public domain do not line up with what product pages advertise. The readiness score folds those surfaces in with crawler rules and live product markup so you review one checklist before launch or AI shopping discovery.

How to use the policy / shipping / tax / domain slice

  1. Run the readiness score so policy, shipping, tax, domain, and product data are included with crawler and markup checks.
  2. Open the fix guidance for any policy, shipping, tax, or domain gaps the score flags.
  3. Repair the live storefront surfaces (published policies, shipping promises, tax configuration, canonical domain).
  4. Rerun the score after edits; use scheduled scans with email alerts on score changes when you need drift watch.
