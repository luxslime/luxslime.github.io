FIC storage conditions

Storage conditions are a before-purchase FIC gap — not only allergens and nutrition

EU Food Info Guard (Shopify App Store) reviews allergen, ingredient, nutrition, net quantity, origin, operator, and storage gaps before food product display. This page is the storage-conditions gap job — not the allergen Annex II detection sibling. The app does not decide whether a product page is lawful. This page is not legal advice.

Storage conditions are a before-purchase surface — not a packing-slip afterthought

Allergen and nutrition lines get attention first. Storage conditions (how the food must be kept) are still part of the same before-display review. When storage guidance is missing or contradictory on the product page, treat it as a gap to close before shoppers buy.

How to review storage-condition gaps

  1. Run the food-info review so storage is included with allergen, ingredient, nutrition, net quantity, origin, and operator checks.
  2. Open any storage gap the review flags on the product.
  3. Add or correct storage-condition wording on the live product page.
  4. Re-run the review so the gap list matches what shoppers see before purchase.
