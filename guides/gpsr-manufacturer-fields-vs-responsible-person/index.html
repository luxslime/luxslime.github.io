GPSR: manufacturer and responsible-person fields versus appointing an EU responsible person

A US Shopify merchant who retypes the same manufacturer and responsible-person fields on every product can put those fields on a vendor metaobject and render them on the product template. That stops the retyping. It does not appoint an EU responsible person.

This page is not legal advice. It does not make the store compliant. It does not make a file legally sufficient.

What the vendor metaobject actually changes

Use a vendor metaobject when the same manufacturer and responsible-person text belongs on many products:

vendor (metaobject)
manufacturer name / address / contact
responsible-person name / address / contact

product template
renders those fields from the attached vendor

each product
attaches that vendor once
  • Retyping stops after the vendor record exists and the template reads it.
  • A product with no vendor attached still has no fields to render.
  • Changing the vendor record updates every product that uses it. Confirm the live product page after you change it.

That is storefront copy you chose to show. It is not an appointment, and it is not a legal file.

When the representative quote is more than EU revenue

If a representative quote is more than EU revenue, merchants often turn the country off. That is a commercial choice about whether the sales cover the quote. This page does not decide that, and turning a country off is not a compliance certificate.

Shipping into the United Kingdom is a different control from EU markets. Shipping zones can exclude Northern Ireland without a second market:

  • Keep a UK zone.
  • Open regions.
  • Clear Northern Ireland only.

That excludes NI from that zone. It does not appoint anyone, and it does not stand in for GPSR fields on products you still sell into the EU.

Do not mix this with EUDR

GPSR manufacturer and responsible-person fields are not EUDR. EUDR is deforestation due diligence: operator role, relevant products, and lot records such as a DDS reference. Keep those jobs apart.

If you need the EUDR dates and record split, use who is in scope on 30 December 2026 vs mid-2027. That page does not appoint an EU responsible person either.

What to do on the catalog

  1. Put manufacturer and responsible-person fields on a vendor metaobject once.
  2. Render those fields on the product template.
  3. Attach that vendor to each product once. Do not retype the same block on every SKU.
  4. If a representative quote is more than EU revenue, decide whether to turn the country off. That decision is yours.
  5. If the UK zone should not include Northern Ireland, use regions and clear NI only. You do not need a second market for that exclusion.

Does a vendor metaobject stop me retyping manufacturer and responsible-person fields on every product?

Yes, for the typing. Put the manufacturer and responsible-person fields on a vendor metaobject, then render those fields on the product template. You still attach that vendor to each product once. You do not retype the same name, address, and contact on every SKU.

Does that appoint an EU responsible person?

No. Showing fields you already store is catalog work. Appointing an EU responsible person is a separate legal step. A metaobject, a theme block, or a file you keep does not make that appointment.

Does that make a file or product page legally sufficient?

No. Rendering manufacturer and responsible-person fields does not make a file legally sufficient and does not make the store compliant. This page is not legal advice and does not certify GPSR.

What if the representative quote is more than EU revenue?

Merchants often turn the country off. That is a commercial choice about whether EU sales cover the quote. It is not a compliance certification, and this page does not decide that for you.

Can shipping zones exclude Northern Ireland without a second market?

Yes. Use a UK shipping zone, open regions, and clear Northern Ireland only. That excludes NI from that zone without adding a second market. Markets and shipping zones are different controls.

Is this the same as EUDR?

No. GPSR manufacturer and responsible-person fields are not EUDR lot records or deforestation due diligence. Use EUDR scope: 30 December 2026 vs mid-2027 for that job. The two pages are different.
