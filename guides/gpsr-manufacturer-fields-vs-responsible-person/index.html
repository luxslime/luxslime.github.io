What the GPSR vendor-field guide is not

The GPSR vendor-field guide covers catalog typing only: manufacturer and responsible-person fields on a vendor metaobject, rendered on the product template after the vendor is attached once. The guide does not appoint an EU responsible person, certify a file, or cover EUDR lot records, CLP classification, textile fibre names, or food allergen labelling.

Can I stop retyping manufacturer and responsible-person fields on every product?

Yes, for the typing: a merchant puts manufacturer and responsible-person fields on a vendor metaobject, renders those fields on the product template, and attaches that vendor to each product once so the same name, address, and contact are not retyped on every SKU. The GPSR vendor-field guide does not appoint an EU responsible person.

Does a vendor metaobject appoint an EU responsible person?

No: showing stored manufacturer and responsible-person fields is catalog work, while appointing an EU responsible person is a separate legal step. A vendor metaobject, a theme block, or a file a merchant keeps does not make that appointment and does not make a file legally sufficient.

Does rendering those fields make a file or product page legally sufficient?

No: rendering manufacturer and responsible-person fields on the product template does not make a file legally sufficient, and the GPSR vendor-field guide does not say the store is compliant. Appointing an EU responsible person remains a separate legal step and is not legal advice from the guide.

What if the representative quote is more than EU revenue?

Merchants often turn the country off when a representative quote is more than EU revenue, and turning the country off is a commercial choice, not a compliance certificate. The GPSR vendor-field guide does not appoint an EU responsible person and does not decide whether a store should sell into a market.

Can shipping zones exclude Northern Ireland without a second market?

Yes: a merchant uses a UK shipping zone, opens regions, and clears Northern Ireland only, which excludes NI from that zone without a second market. Shipping-zone region settings are not an EU responsible-person appointment and are not a compliance certificate.

Is GPSR manufacturer-field work the same as EUDR?

No: GPSR manufacturer and responsible-person fields are catalog contact fields, not EUDR lot records, so deforestation due diligence dates and DDS references stay on EUDR scope: 30 December 2026 vs mid-2027. The two jobs stay on separate pages.
