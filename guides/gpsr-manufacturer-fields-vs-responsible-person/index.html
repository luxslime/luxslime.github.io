GPSR manufacturer fields

Stop retyping GPSR manufacturer and responsible-person fields on every product

Start path: one vendor record, then attach once

  1. Put manufacturer and responsible-person fields on a vendor metaobject.
  2. Render those fields on the product template.
  3. Attach that vendor to each product once.
  4. Open the live product page and confirm the fields render.

A product with no vendor attached has nothing to render. Changing the vendor record updates every product that uses it. Confirm the live page after you change it.

Can I stop retyping manufacturer and responsible-person fields on every product?

Yes, for the typing. Put the fields on a vendor metaobject. Render them on the product template. Attach that vendor to each product once. You do not retype the same name, address, and contact on every SKU.

Does a vendor metaobject appoint an EU responsible person?

No. Showing stored fields is catalog work. Appointing an EU responsible person is a separate legal step. A metaobject, a theme block, or a file you keep does not make that appointment.

Does that make a file or product page legally sufficient?

No. Rendering the fields does not make a file legally sufficient. This page does not say the store is compliant. It is not legal advice.

What if the representative quote is more than EU revenue?

Merchants often turn the country off. That is a commercial choice. It is not a compliance certificate.

Can shipping zones exclude Northern Ireland without a second market?

Yes. Use a UK shipping zone, open regions, and clear Northern Ireland only. That excludes NI from that zone. You do not need a second market for that exclusion.

Is this the same as EUDR?

No. GPSR manufacturer and responsible-person fields are not EUDR lot records. Use EUDR scope: 30 December 2026 vs mid-2027 for that job. The two pages are different.
