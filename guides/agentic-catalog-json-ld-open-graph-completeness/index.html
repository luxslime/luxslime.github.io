JSON-LD and Open Graph audit

A theme that emits some Product JSON-LD is not a catalog-wide JSON-LD and Open Graph completeness audit

Direct answer. Shopify themes can emit Product JSON-LD and you can set Open Graph tags in the theme editor, but Admin has no catalog-wide audit that checks Product JSON-LD and Open Graph completeness on live product pages in one pass. Agentic Catalog audits Product JSON-LD and Open Graph completeness on live product pages so you can see which products are incomplete before AI shopping discovery.

Agentic Catalog by momiji (Shopify App Store) audits Product JSON-LD and Open Graph completeness on live product pages, reviews policy, shipping, tax, domain, and product data in one readiness score, and reviews fix guidance so you can rerun scans. This page is the JSON-LD and Open Graph completeness slice — not the readiness-score policy bundle or fix-guidance reruns alone. This page is not legal advice.

Why completeness on live pages matters

Theme defaults and a single product preview do not prove every live URL emits complete Product JSON-LD and Open Graph tags. A catalog-wide live-page audit is a different job from editing one theme template.

How to audit JSON-LD and Open Graph completeness

  1. Run an audit across live product pages for Product JSON-LD and Open Graph fields.
  2. Open incomplete products and fix the missing fields or theme output.
  3. Rerun the scan after fixes.
  4. Keep readiness score and policy checks as a separate follow-on if you need the broader score.
