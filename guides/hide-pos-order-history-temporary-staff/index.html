Temporary and seasonal POS staff

Temporary POS staff still see that location’s past orders — there is no hide-history permission

Direct answer. Shopify’s Point of Sale permissions table has no permission that hides past order history. Temporary or seasonal staff who can take a sale still see orders made at the device’s location. The closest control is Manage orders at all locations set to Denied, which Shopify documents as restricting staff to orders made at the device’s location — not as hiding that location’s history. Return and exchange orders is a separate refund control; turning it off does not hide orders. Confirm the live Help Center pages; Shopify can change labels. This page is not legal advice and not a privacy attestation.

Staff Access Audit Guard (Shopify App Store) reviews installed-app access, granted scopes, staff access records, and ownership evidence without reading customer or order data. It tracks staff and collaborator access with 30/90-day review reminders. It does not revoke staff, change POS roles, or hide order history. What native Shopify can and cannot do

Native Shopify can assign a POS role with named register permissions. Shopify documents that you assign a role, not individual POS permissions, on Understanding Shopify POS staff management. POS roles are documented for some location subscriptions. On that role you can deny Manage orders at all locations so staff manage only orders made at the device’s location, and you can deny Return and exchange orders so they cannot refund. You can also deny View customer details, which Shopify describes as restricting the customer’s email, phone, and shipping address on POS — that is customer contact fields, not the order list.

Native Shopify cannot hide that location’s past orders from a staff member who is ringing sales. The orders permission set on the Help Center page does not include a “view order history” or “hide past orders” row. Customer privacy for past purchases is therefore not a POS toggle you missed.

Checklist: limit what temporary staff can do

  • Open the POS role Shopify documents, not only Settings > Users admin roles. Admin permissions and POS roles are different records — see POS roles vs admin staff permissions.
  • Set Manage orders at all locations to Denied so seasonal staff do not browse other locations’ orders.
  • Set Return and exchange orders to Denied unless that person should refund.
  • Review View customer details if the job does not need email, phone, or shipping address on the register.
  • When the shift block ends, suspend or remove the user in Settings > Users. There is no calendar end date on the POS role — that gap is set an end date on staff or collaborator access.
