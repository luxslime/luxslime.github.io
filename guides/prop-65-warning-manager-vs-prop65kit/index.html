Prop 65 app comparison

Prop 65 Warning Manager vs Prop65Kit — approved, cited warnings vs theme-editor display

Direct answer. Choose Prop65Kit when you want to add the California Proposition 65 safe-harbor warning from the Shopify theme editor with no code, in short or long format, on every product or only on items you tag. Choose Prop 65 Warning Manager when every warning needs merchant approval before it goes live, published from templates citing 27 CCR 25601–25607 including 2028 short-form text with chemical names, with each pick, edit, and activation timestamped in an audit log.

How to choose

If you want to add the California Proposition 65 safe-harbor warning from the Shopify theme editor with no code, in short or long format, on every product or only on items you tag, Prop65Kit fits that job. If every warning needs merchant approval before it goes live, published from templates citing 27 CCR 25601–25607 including 2028 short-form text with chemical names, with each pick, edit, and activation timestamped in an audit log, Prop 65 Warning Manager fits that job.
