Catalog readiness for AI shopping agents

AI shopping agents skip catalogs without crawler rules and complete product JSON-LD

Agentic Catalog by momiji (Shopify App Store) is a Shopify app that checks llms.txt and robots.txt rules for GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot, audits Product JSON-LD and Open Graph completeness on live product pages, and reviews policy, shipping, tax, domain and product data in one readiness score. It does not compare Admin values per variant, market or currency, and it cannot see what an AI assistant answered.

Job: make the catalog machine-readable before an AI shopping agent tries to discover it: confirm the default llms.txt says what you want, set GPTBot / ClaudeBot / PerplexityBot in robots.txt on purpose, and put complete Product JSON-LD (and Open Graph) on live product pages.

Does my Shopify store already have llms.txt?

Yes. By default since 28 May 2026, every Shopify store includes /agents.md, and /llms.txt and /llms-full.txt point to that content. You do not need a third-party app to generate these files. Themes can override them with agents.md.liquid, llms.txt.liquid, or llms-full.txt.liquid. Step-by-step override: customize agents.md and llms.txt with theme templates. See the 28 May 2026 Shopify changelog and the Help Center on Shopify Catalog and agentic storefront products.

The readiness questions are whether that default content says what you want (customize via agents.md.liquid), whether your robots.txt AI-crawler lines are deliberate, and whether live Product JSON-LD and Open Graph are complete. robots.txt blocks do not stop Shopify Catalog from sending product data to activated agentic storefronts.

Do my products need GTINs for AI shopping agents?

A barcode is listed as an input, not as an eligibility requirement. Shopify Help on optimizing your products for AI platforms says the fields AI platforms consider include Title, Description, Images, product organization (Type, Vendor, Collections, and Tags), Barcode (ISBN, UPC, GTIN, etc.), and Variants (including Option name). That list is not a ranking promise.

A variant can hold up to 20 barcodes. Channels read the first one — put the GS1 GTIN first if you also store Custom or internal codes. Dedicated first-barcode, GTIN length, API, and theme JSON-LD notes: Shopify GTIN / barcode for AI shopping agents.

The readiness scan flags missing identifiers in live Product JSON-LD. Repair barcode/GTIN only where the listing marks that path live. This page does not claim support for multiple barcodes, barcode types, or NS_PID. A readiness score is a checklist. It does not enroll the store in any agent storefront, and it is not a ranking or inclusion promise.

What does “catalog ready for AI shopping agents” mean on this page?

It means a crawler can find the store’s machine-readable catalog rules and a product page exposes complete product structured data. The checklist on this page is llms.txt, robots.txt rules for GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot, plus Product JSON-LD and Open Graph on live product pages. It is not a promise that any agent will recommend or buy the product.

Is a robots.txt allow enough without llms.txt?

No. robots.txt tells specific crawlers whether they may fetch. llms.txt is a separate, public hint about which URLs and context an LLM-facing agent should prefer. Shopify stores already serve llms.txt by default; the gap is whether that default content says what you want, not whether the file exists. Confirm what you actually publish at both paths.

Does complete JSON-LD make the catalog “agentic”?

No. JSON-LD completeness (Product, offers, identifiers) is one input to a readiness score. Policy, shipping, tax, and domain checks sit beside it. Barcode/GTIN repair may be live while price, availability, or ProductGroup stay preview-only until a paid unlock. Preview is not a live catalog fix.

Will a high readiness score get the store into ChatGPT shopping or similar?

No. A score is an internal checklist against crawler rules and on-page structured data. Agent storefronts, merchant of record, and feed partnerships are separate programs. This page is not a ranking, inclusion, or sales guarantee.

How often should I re-scan?

Whenever you change robots.txt, llms.txt, or product identifiers, and on a daily or weekly schedule if you need email alerts when the score moves. A one-time scan goes stale the next time a theme or feed drops JSON-LD.

Is this the same job as auditing AI apps that can read customer data?

No. Staff and installed-app scope risk is an access audit. Catalog readiness is public crawler and structured-data hygiene. Do not treat a robots.txt allow as permission for an installed app to read orders.

Where do I score crawler rules and product JSON-LD?

Use a readiness scan that checks llms.txt, AI-crawler robots.txt, and Product JSON-LD / Open Graph on live product pages, then exports per-product evidence.
