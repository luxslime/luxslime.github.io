Compostable / biodegradable claims

Compostable and biodegradable wording on the product page is a cited green-claim scan — not a freehand adjective

Green Claims Guard (Shopify App Store) scans green marketing wording in your catalog and content, flags green-claim wording and names the exact provision it cites, and exports dated evidence records for review. Compostable and biodegradable lines are the same job as other specific environmental claims: scan the copy, review the cited flag, then keep or rewrite the wording. The app does not decide whether a claim is lawful. This page is not legal advice.

Compostable and biodegradable are specific claim words — not a synonym for “eco”

Generic adjectives (“eco-friendly”, “green”, “sustainable”) are a different EmpCo / generic-claim job. Compostable and biodegradable name a disposal or breakdown pathway. When those words appear on product copy, treat them as claim wording to scan and cite, not as decoration. Pair this with dated evidence records when you keep a specific claim.

How to run the compostable / biodegradable scan

  1. Scan product and content copy for green-claim wording, including compostable and biodegradable lines.
  2. Review each flag and the exact provision the scan cites.
  3. Rewrite, qualify, or drop wording you cannot support; keep dated evidence records for claims you publish.
  4. Re-scan after edits so the live copy matches what you approved.
