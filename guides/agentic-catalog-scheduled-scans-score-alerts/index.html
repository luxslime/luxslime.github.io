Scheduled readiness scans

AI catalog readiness drifts — schedule scans and get alerts when the score changes

Direct answer. Shopify Admin does not publish a catalog readiness score for AI shopping agents, and it does not email you when llms.txt, robots.txt, or Product JSON-LD completeness changes on the live storefront. Agentic Catalog checks llms.txt and robots.txt for GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot, audits Product JSON-LD and Open Graph on live product pages, and schedules daily or weekly scans with email alerts on score changes.

Agentic Catalog by momiji (Shopify App Store) checks llms.txt and robots.txt for GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot, audits Product JSON-LD and Open Graph on live product pages, reviews policy, shipping, tax, domain, and product data in one readiness score, and schedules daily or weekly scans with email alerts on score changes. This page is the scheduled-scan / alert job. This page is not legal advice.

A one-shot readiness score goes stale when the catalog changes

AI crawler rules, Product JSON-LD, and Open Graph completeness drift when you edit products or theme head tags. Scheduled daily or weekly scans with email alerts on score changes catch that drift without waiting for the next manual rerun.

How to schedule scans and watch score changes

  1. Run the readiness score once so crawler rules, Product JSON-LD, Open Graph, and policy / shipping / tax / domain inputs are baseline.
  2. Schedule daily or weekly scans.
  3. Watch email alerts when the readiness score changes.
  4. Open the fix guidance, repair the live surfaces, and confirm the next scan.
