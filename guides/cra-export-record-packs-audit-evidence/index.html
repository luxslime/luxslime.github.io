CRA record pack export

CRA duty work needs exportable record packs and a chronological audit trail — not a scattered file folder

Direct answer. Shopify Admin can hold product metafields and Files attachments, but it does not export a CRA duty record pack with chronological audit evidence across roles, gaps, and incident clocks. CRALedger Duty Records exports record packs and chronological audit evidence so you can review the trail in one place.

CRALedger Duty Records (Shopify App Store) tracks product roles, checklists and open record gaps, Article 14 incident deadlines, encrypted ENISA SRP draft packs, vulnerability forwarding, and audit records — including exportable record packs and chronological audit evidence. This page is the export/audit-evidence slice — not Article 14 clocks, ENISA drafts, open-gap checklists, or vulnerability forwarding alone. This page is not legal advice.

A Files folder is not chronological audit evidence

Scattered PDFs do not show when a gap closed, when an Article 14 clock moved, or which role checklist produced the row. Exportable packs plus a chronological trail are the review job.

How to export CRA record packs and audit evidence

  1. Keep product roles, checklists, and open gaps current in the duty record.
  2. Record Article 14 and related events so the chronology stays intact.
  3. Export a record pack when you need a reviewable bundle.
  4. Use the chronological audit evidence to see what changed and when.
