ENISA SRP draft packs

Encrypted ENISA SRP draft packs prepare Article 14 filings — they do not submit them for you

CRALedger Duty Records (Shopify App Store) tracks CRA product duties, Article 14 incident deadlines, encrypted ENISA SRP draft packs, vulnerability forwarding, and audit records. Record product roles and checklists, flag open gaps, track Article 14 clocks, and prepare encrypted ENISA Single Reporting Platform drafts you can export. When you must notify, submit on ENISA’s Single Reporting Platform — not inside the catalog app. This page is not legal advice.

A draft pack is not the same job as filing on ENISA’s platform

Article 14 clocks (early warning, follow-up, final report) are one surface. An encrypted ENISA SRP draft pack is the prepared payload you can export under those clocks. Filing still happens on ENISA’s Single Reporting Platform. The app does not replace that submission step.

How to prepare SRP drafts beside Article 14 clocks

  1. Record the product role and checklist; flag open record gaps.
  2. Track Article 14 incident deadlines when you become aware of active exploitation or a severe incident.
  3. Prepare encrypted ENISA SRP draft packs for export.
  4. Submit on ENISA’s Single Reporting Platform when you must notify; keep chronological audit evidence.
