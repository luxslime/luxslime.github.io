Agentic Catalog ・ ChatGPT ・ Shopify Catalog

Do Shopify merchants need a separate OpenAI product feed for ChatGPT?

Direct answer. No — not for Shopify Catalog syndication to ChatGPT. Shopify Help (Selling on ChatGPT) says you do not need to do anything for products to be discovered by ChatGPT through Shopify Catalog when the store and products are eligible. OpenAI Help for Shopify merchants points merchants to Shopify’s Agentic Storefronts guidance rather than a separate feed upload. OpenAI’s Get Started feed onboarding is for approved partners who share a structured product feed (nine required fields, SFTP) — that path is different from Shopify Catalog. Agentic Catalog by momiji does not upload an OpenAI feed and does not replace Catalog; it audits live-store discovery files and structured data (llms.txt, AI crawler robots, Product JSON-LD / Open Graph) and can schedule scans or export CSV evidence. Listing: Free (5 scans/mo); Pro $9.99/mo scheduled + alerts; $24.99/mo CSV.

Why merchants ask this

Search and Community language mixes “ChatGPT shopping feed,” “OpenAI product feed,” “AI shopping feed,” and “Shopify Catalog.” Our ChatGPT shopping and OpenAI product feed spec pages describe the approved-partner feed. Shopify merchants often need the opposite answer first: Catalog already syndicates eligible product data.

Two paths, one confusion

PathWho it is forWhat you do
Shopify Catalog → ChatGPTEligible Shopify storesMeet Catalog / ChatGPT requirements in admin; no separate OpenAI feed file

Shopify Catalog Help also notes that being in Catalog does not guarantee appearance in every AI answer. Checkout for ChatGPT happens on your online store checkout (in-app browser or new tab) — there is no ChatGPT direct-checkout toggle in Agentic admin.

When the partner feed pages still matter

If you are an approved OpenAI commerce partner outside the Shopify Catalog path, follow the nine required fields and delivery model on OpenAI product feed spec. If you only run a Shopify store and Catalog shows eligible, start with eligibility and product data quality, not an SFTP feed.

What to verify instead of building a feed

  1. Sales channels → Agentic → ChatGPT Catalog access and Sources → Shopify Catalog (count vs Ineligible).
  2. Store policies completed; US selling; Catalog-eligible products (Shopify Help).
  3. Live product completeness; optional Catalog Mapping when titles/descriptions live in metafields.
  4. Crawl-side readiness: /llms.txt, robots for AI crawlers, Product JSON-LD / Open Graph — run an Agentic Catalog scan.
