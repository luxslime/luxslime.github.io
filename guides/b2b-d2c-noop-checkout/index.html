B2B D2C no-op

Required PO and B2B checkout rules must no-op on D2C — company checkout is a different surface

B2B PO Guard — Require PO (Shopify App Store) validates purchase-order numbers by format, prefix, regex, length, and uniqueness, sets deposit percentage guardrails, routes orders above a threshold for review, and previews any PO rule live against a sample number before you publish. D2C no-op behavior keeps retail checkout on the ordinary path. Checkout enforcement runs on Plus B2B; PO rules work on every plan. This page is not legal advice.

D2C and no-company carts should stay on ordinary checkout

A required PO string, a deposit percentage, or an order-review hold belongs on company / B2B checkout. Retail D2C and no-company carts should not see that wholesale PO field. If a retail buyer hits a PO rule, the rule is on the wrong surface — that is the D2C no-op job alongside PO validation.

Sequence: preview on a sample, publish, then test company vs D2C

  1. Build the PO rule (format, prefix, regex, length, uniqueness as needed).
  2. Preview the rule live against a sample number before you publish.
  3. Confirm checkout enforcement for a company cart (junk PO errors; matching PO passes).
  4. Confirm a D2C / no-company cart stays on ordinary checkout with no PO block.
