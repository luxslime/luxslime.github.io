EUDR due diligence statement example

EUDR due diligence statement: Annex II fields, with an illustrative example

Direct answer. Under Regulation (EU) 2023/1115 Article 4, operators must not place relevant products on the market or export them without first submitting a due diligence statement through the information system in Article 33. That statement must contain the information in Annex II plus a declaration that due diligence found no or only a negligible risk. The table below is an illustrative reading aid of those Annex II headings — not a real statement, not a filing form, and not a substitute for the Information System. This page is not legal advice.

EUDR Records Manager (Shopify App Store) keeps a supplier and commodity-lot registry with DDS reference numbers per product, flags missing supplier, country or DDS fields, and retains an audit trail of supplier, lot, document and export changes. It does not file the due diligence statement in the EU information system. Storing the reference you received is EUDR DDS lot registry.

What the regulation says a statement contains

Article 4(2) of Regulation (EU) 2023/1115 as consolidated requires an electronically available statement with the Annex II information and a declaration that the operator exercised due diligence and that no or only a negligible risk was found. Operators keep a record of those statements for five years from the date of submission. Confirm the live articles, including amendments, before you treat any SKU as in scope. Dates and roles are on who is in scope on 30 December 2026 vs mid-2027.

Annex II currently lists, in paraphrase:

  • Operator name, address, and (when products enter or leave the market) the EORI number
  • Harmonised System code, free-text description including the trade name and, where applicable, the full scientific name, and quantity
  • Country of production and the geolocation of all plots of land where the relevant commodities were produced (with the cattle-establishment rule in the same point)
  • The confirmation text set out in Annex II
  • A signature block in the format Annex II specifies

Read the live Annex II text; this list is a reading aid. A later amendment struck a former Annex II point, so numbering on older copies may not match the current annex.

Where the official Information System guidance lives

The Commission describes the Information System as a TRACES tool in which operators and authorised representatives create and submit due diligence statements and simplified declarations. Official pages to open:

  • The Information System of the Deforestation Regulation — production vs acceptance servers, geolocation options, dashboard, API note, and the user guide link
  • Understand due diligence — points to Annex II as the DDS template and to the Information System guide
  • Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/3084 — functioning of the Information System, including assignment of a reference number and a verification number

The Green Forum Information System page states that submissions on the production server have legal value and can be checked by competent authorities, and that the acceptance server is for training and testing only. This website is neither server.

Storing a reference is not filing a statement

Operators submit the statement in the Information System and receive a reference number. Downstream operators and traders keep the reference they received. A catalog lot registry stores SKU or lot, supplier, country, and that reference. EUDR Records Manager stores records you enter; it does not file a statement or replace the Information System. Start path: store the DDS reference against each supplier lot.

What this page is not

  • Not the lot-registry start path. That is DDS reference numbers on supplier lots.
