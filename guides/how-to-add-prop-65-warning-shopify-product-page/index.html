How to add a Prop 65 warning on a Shopify product page

Direct answer. To put a California Proposition 65 warning on a Shopify product page with an approval gate — not a theme paste alone — install Prop 65 Warning Manager (App Store: momiji-prop-65-warning-manager), pick a 27 CCR template (including 2028 short-form text with chemical names), assign it to the product, and require merchant approval before go-live. Every pick, edit, and activation is timestamped in an audit log; CSV export is on a paid plan. Shopify also ships native Disclosures metaobjects for Prop 65 wording; those display warnings but do not replace an approval-and-audit workflow. Listing: Free (2028 form + chemical + OK, no audit/CSV); Starter $29/mo; Growth $59/mo. Free trial on paid plans.

Why merchants ask this

Sellers still search “prop 65 warning shopify product page” and ask how to show compliant text without burying it in the description. Public examples checked on 2026-10-30 include Reddit r/shopify (“I am asked to be California Prop 65 compliant… on Shopify”), r/ecommerce “Prop65 Warnings?”, and Shopify Help’s product disclosures docs for native Prop 65 entries. This page is the product-page how-to with approval — not the 2028 chemical-name deep dive alone, and not cart-only or ship-to-California checkout controls.

Steps with Prop 65 Warning Manager

  1. Install the app from the Shopify App Store listing above.
  2. Choose a cited template (27 CCR §§ 25601–25607), including 2028 short-form text that names the chemical when that form applies.
  3. Assign the warning to the catalog products that need it.
  4. Require merchant approval before the warning goes live on the product page.
  5. Confirm the storefront shows the warning on the product template; keep the audit trail of picks, edits, and activations. Export CSV on Growth.

Native Shopify Disclosures (display path)

Shopify Help: Products → product metafields → Disclosures can attach a Prop 65 chemical-exposure entry, set jurisdictions (for example US-CA), and enable product-page (and cart) display preferences. Themes need a Disclosures block where supported. Native display is not the same as merchant approval plus audit log — see the native-vs-audit sibling if that is your question.
