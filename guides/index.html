Prop 65

Prop 65 at checkout when shipping to California (not IP geo)

Ship-to-California Prop 65 is a checkout shipping-address job, not US-CA on the disclosure or an IP pop-up. Native path first. Not legal advice.

Prop 65 audit trail of picks, edits, and go-live

Shopify native disclosures do not keep an audit trail of warning picks, edits, and go-live. Prop 65 Warning Manager records those events. Not legal advice.

Prop 65 food short-form warning on Shopify (vs chemical products)

Food Prop 65 short-form uses /food and no triangle. That is warning content, not a separate Shopify disclosure type. Templates, approval, and activation audit. Not legal advice.

Prop 65 merchant approval before a warning goes live

Prop 65 Warning Manager requires merchant approval before any warning goes live and timestamps picks, edits, and activations in an audit trail. Not a theme-only display tool. Not legal advice.

Prop 65 storefront activation audit on Shopify

A storefront activation record is not the same job as typing a warning into the theme. Approve before go-live; keep activation events in the audit trail. Not legal advice.

Prop 65 warning assignment across the Shopify catalog

Find which Shopify products have a Prop 65 warning: filter the product list by disclosure type, and keep approval before any warning goes live. Not legal advice.

Prop 65 warning on the product page — chemical name for 2028

Prop 65 warning for goods labeled on or after 1 Jan 2028 needs a listed chemical name on the product page. Install Prop 65 Warning Manager, attach the short form, and match the label. Not legal advice.

Prop 65 warning templates citing 27 CCR on Shopify

Publish Prop 65 warnings from templates that cite 27 CCR §§ 25601–25607, including 2028 short-form text with chemical names. Approve before go-live; keep the audit trail. Not legal advice.

Shopify Prop 65 alcohol disclosure — wine, beer, spirits

Wine, beer, and spirits use Shopify’s Prop 65 alcohol disclosure type, not cancer / reproductive. Native path first. Not legal advice.

Shopify Prop 65 on the cart vs product page

A Prop 65 product-page disclosure is a separate Display preference from the cart. Native Disclosures can target both. Approval and storefront-activation audit are a different job. Not legal advice.

Show a Prop 65 warning only for California (US-CA) on Shopify

California-only Prop 65 on Shopify is a US-CA jurisdiction on structured Disclosures, not an IP pop-up. Native path first. Not legal advice.

An SDS download link is not CLP hazard display on the product page

A Safety Data Sheet link is not the same job as showing CLP pictograms and statements on the product page. Record hazard profiles, sync metafields, review missing fields before publish.

CLP hazard pictograms on the product page, not the parcel

Show CLP/GHS hazard pictograms and H/P statements on the product page before purchase. The app does not classify the mixture. Not legal advice.

Create CLP hazard profiles with GHS pictograms and statements

Shopify has no built-in CLP hazard profile. CLPLens creates product hazard profiles with GHS pictograms and statements, then syncs metafields for storefront display. Not legal advice.

Review missing CLP hazard fields before you publish

Gap-check GHS pictograms, signal word, and H/P statements before the CLP product-page block goes live. CLPLens: CLP Hazard Display. Not legal advice.

Sync CLP hazard display through app-owned product metafields

Shopify has no built-in CLP hazard block. CLPLens syncs app-owned product metafields for theme-block hazard display and keeps audit records. Not legal advice.

UFI on Shopify product pages for EU CLP distance sales (from 2028)

From 1 January 2028, a CLP distance-sale offer must clearly show Art. 17 label elements — including the UFI where the pack already needs it. Not a UFI generator. Not legal advice.

B2B PO

B2B checkout can skip a PO number unless a rule requires it

Require PO number format, prefix, and uniqueness on B2B checkout. Native Net Terms do not check that. Preview a sample number before you publish.

B2B PO and deposit rules work alongside native Net Terms

Shopify native B2B Net Terms set payment due dates per company location. B2B Terms & PO Guard adds PO and deposit rules alongside those terms; D2C stays untouched. Not legal advice.

Prevent duplicate PO numbers on Shopify B2B checkout

Stop reused PO numbers on Shopify B2B checkout: add uniqueness to the PO validation rule and preview it against a known duplicate before you publish.

Preview a Shopify B2B PO rule before you publish

Preview any PO rule live against a sample number before you publish. D2C stays untouched. Checkout enforcement runs on Plus B2B; PO rules work on every plan. Not legal advice.

Shopify B2B deposit percentage at checkout (vs Net Terms and PO)

A deposit % on B2B checkout is not Net Terms and not a required PO number. Set deposit guardrails and optional order review. D2C stays untouched.

Shopify B2B order review threshold vs PO and Net Terms

Holding oversized B2B orders for approval is not a required PO and not Net Terms. Route company orders above a threshold for manual review. D2C stays untouched.

Shopify B2B PO rules should no-op on D2C checkout

Required PO and B2B checkout rules must no-op on D2C. Company checkout is a different surface. Preview PO rules. Checkout enforcement runs on Plus B2B; PO rules work on every plan. Not legal advice.

Validate B2B PO format, prefix, regex, and length

Shopify has no built-in no-code PO format setting. A Cart and Checkout Validation function can read cart.poNumber. B2B Terms & PO Guard checks format, prefix, regex, length, and uniqueness. Not legal advice.

PO Box / AddressFence

PO box blocker for the billing address, not just shipping

PO box blocker for Shopify billing addresses, plus the street-number rule Address Blocker dropped. Native checkout rules cover shipping only.

Require a street number at Shopify checkout after the Address Blocker sunset

Shopify's address format validation can't require a street number. Additional address fields cover only some countries. Here is the gap and a fix.

Shopify built-in checkout address rules cannot target billing

Shopify built-in, no-code checkout settings do not target billing. A validation function such as AddressFence can read billingAddress. Not legal advice.

Shopify require billing match shipping is not a PO Box block

Requiring shipping and billing to match only forces identical addresses. It does not detect a PO Box. AddressFence adds billing-address rules and street-number checks at checkout.

Test a Shopify address rule in a simulator before you publish

Native Checkout Blocks address format validation covers shipping only. Test billing PO box and street-number rules in a simulator before you publish, with one-click rollback.

PO box spellings and look-alike street names: test your Shopify billing rule first

A PO box rule can miss a spelling or stop a real street like Boxwood Road. A test list to run through the AddressFence simulator before you publish.

Textile / FibreLedger

EU textile fibre composition language on Shopify (Art. 16(3))

Art. 16(3): fibre composition must be in the official language(s) of the Member State of availability. Annex I names alone are not enough. Not legal advice.

EU textile fibre labels need Annex I names, not trade names

Validate fibre names against Annex I. Flag trade names such as Lycra. Record French places of manufacture on the product page. Not legal advice.

Flag Lycra and Tencel trade names on Shopify fibre fields

Shopify has no built-in checker that flags Lycra or Tencel on fibre composition fields. FibreLedger flags trade names that are not lawful Annex I fibre names. Not legal advice.

France places of manufacture vs EU fibre names (Annex I): two separate textile duties on Shopify

French places of manufacture and Annex I fibre names are two separate textile duties on Shopify. Recording origin is not a fibre-composition substitute. FibreLedger Textile Labels. Not legal advice.

France: show places of manufacture next to the price on Shopify (Code de l'environnement L. 541-9-1-2)

Since 10 July 2026, French online listings for clothing, footwear and household linen must show places of manufacture near the price, same text size.

Record textile environmental-impact information on Shopify

Shopify has no built-in textile environmental-impact fields. FibreLedger records environmental-impact information alongside Annex I fibre checks. Not legal advice.

Textile EPR registration and audit records on Shopify

FibreLedger Textile Labels retains EPR registration and audit records for textile sellers, alongside Annex I fibre-name checks and French origin / environmental-impact fields. Not legal advice.

“Bamboo” on an EU textile label: use the Annex I fibre name (viscose)

Art. 5(1): only Annex I names describe fibre composition. “Bamboo” is not one. Viscose-route bamboo is viscose (Annex I no. 25). Not legal advice.

Green claims

Compostable and biodegradable claims on Shopify product copy

Scan compostable and biodegradable marketing wording on Shopify product copy; Green Claims Guard flags green-claim wording and names the exact provision it cites. Not legal advice.

Dated evidence records for Shopify green claims

Finding eco-friendly wording is not the same as keeping dated evidence for a specific claim. Scan, flag with cites, export dated evidence records. Not legal advice.

EmpCo carbon-neutral claims on Shopify (offset ban vs lifecycle proof)

EmpCo Annex I 4c bans offset-based carbon-neutral or climate-neutral product claims from 27 September 2026. Lifecycle-in-value-chain claims are a different analysis. Not legal advice.

EmpCo recyclable claims on Shopify product pages

"Recyclable" on the product page is not the same job as carbon-neutral offsets. Scan green wording, review cited flags, keep dated evidence. Not legal advice.

EU bans generic green claims on product copy, 27 Sep 2026

Directive 2024/825 bans unspecialised eco-friendly or green wording from 27 September 2026 unless recognised excellent performance applies. Not legal advice.

Flag green-claim wording and see which provision it cites

Shopify has no built-in green-claim provision map. Green Claims Guard flags wording and names the exact provision it cites. Not legal advice.

German UWG green claims on Shopify product pages

Vague German Umweltaussagen (umweltfreundlich, öko, klimaneutral) on a DE-facing Shopify product page need same-medium specification under UWG EmpCo rules from 27 September 2026. Not legal advice.

Made in USA claims on Shopify product pages (FTC)

Unqualified Made in USA on a Shopify product page generally needs all-or-virtually-all U.S. content, or a clear qualified claim. FTC 16 CFR Part 323. Not legal advice.

Recycled-content % claims on Shopify product copy

A bare “recycled” line is not the same as a recycled-content percent on the same page. Scan green wording, review cited flags, keep dated evidence. Not legal advice.

Scan Shopify catalog and content for green-claim wording

Shopify Admin has no catalog-wide green-claim scanner. Green Claims Guard scans catalog and content for eco marketing wording, then you review flags. Not legal advice.

Your own eco badge on a Shopify product page: the EU sustainability-label rule

A self-made eco badge can fall under the EU sustainability-label ban from 27 September 2026 (Annex I, point 2a). What counts and what stays allowed. Not legal advice.

“Net zero by 2030” on a Shopify product page: EU future-performance claims

EU rules from 27 September 2026 judge “net zero by 2030”-style claims case by case: a detailed plan, measurable targets and an independent check. Not legal advice.

“Made with recycled materials” when only the packaging is: the EU whole-product claim rule

Annex I point 4b bans a whole-product environmental claim that covers only one part, such as recycled packaging vs a recycled product. A rewrite pattern for Shopify copy. Not legal advice.

CRA

CRA duty records before an incident — Article 14 product file

CRA duty records for products with digital elements. Keep the Article 14 product file before an incident. Reporting starts 11 September 2026. The app does not file ENISA notifications. Not legal advice.

CRA support period on the Shopify product page (Art. 13(19))

CRA Art. 13(19): show the support-period end date (month and year) at purchase on the Shopify product page. The duty file is not the offer. CRALedger does not render the date. Not legal advice.

CRA vulnerability forwarding vs Article 14 ENISA filing

Importers and distributors inform the manufacturer of a vulnerability. Article 14 ENISA filing is the manufacturer’s job from 11 September 2026. The listing includes vulnerability forwarding. Not legal advice.

Encrypted ENISA SRP draft packs for CRA Article 14

Track CRA Article 14 incident deadlines and prepare encrypted ENISA Single Reporting Platform draft packs. Submit on ENISA’s platform — not inside the catalog app. Not legal advice.

Export CRA record packs and chronological audit evidence

Shopify Admin has no CRA duty-record pack export with chronological audit evidence. CRALedger exports record packs and chronological audit evidence. Not legal advice.

Find open CRA record gaps with product-role checklists

Shopify has no built-in CRA role checklist. CRALedger tracks product roles, checklists, and open record gaps. Not legal advice.

Track CRA Article 14 incident deadlines on the product file

Shopify has no built-in CRA Article 14 deadline tracker. CRALedger tracks Article 14 incident deadlines on the product file. Not legal advice.

EU food info

Best-before date on an EU food product page: not before purchase, yes at delivery

For prepacked food sold at a distance, the best-before or use-by date may wait until delivery; every other mandatory particular belongs on the product page before purchase. Not legal advice.

EU food business operator name and address on Shopify

For EU distance selling, the food business operator’s name and address is a mandatory particular before purchase (Art. 9(1)(h)) — not a GPSR responsible-person field. Not legal advice.

EU food product page: nutrition and net quantity before purchase

For EU distance selling, the nutrition declaration and net quantity belong on the product page before purchase, not only on the parcel. Not legal advice.

EU honey origin on Shopify product pages (blend % from 14 Jun 2026)

From 14 June 2026, honey origin countries and blend percentages belong on the Shopify product page before purchase, not only on the jar. Not legal advice.

Food allergen labels before purchase, not only on delivery

EU FIC requires allergen info before purchase. Detect Annex II allergens from ingredient text and show them on the product page. Not legal advice.

Food storage-condition gaps on Shopify product pages

Shopify descriptions can hold storage text, but Admin does not gap-review food pages. EU Food Info Guard reviews storage gaps before product display. Not legal advice.

Run weekly FIC completeness reviews with an audit trail

Show FIC allergen data with a theme block — no template edits

Staff access

Access Audit 30/90-day review reminders vs collaborator expiry

Shopify’s 90-day collaborator expiry auto-expires unused access. A 30/90-day reminder is a separate cadence; overdue does not expire or Reactivate anyone.

Audit Shopify app scopes for customer data access

Check which installed Shopify apps hold customer-data scopes: review each app under Settings > Apps, or classify and flag every granted scope in one audit.

Audit staff and app access without the audit app reading customer or order data

Run a staff and installed-app access review without the audit app itself reading customer or order data. Staff Access Audit Guard. Not legal advice.

Cannot suspend a Shopify collaborator — remove access instead

Actions → Suspend on a collaborator returns “Cannot suspend or reactivate collaborators.” That is expected. Remove the collaborator account in Settings → Users. Access Audit Guard does not revoke users.

Score installed Shopify app access risk Low to High

Shopify Admin shows per-app permissions, not a Low-to-High access risk score. Staff Access Audit Guard scores every installed app’s access risk. Not legal advice.

Score leftover Admin access: 2FA and publish rights

Score leftover Admin access without installing. Covers leftover 2FA, collaborator billing, and who can publish. No Admin scrape. Not a security attestation.

Shopify app ownership evidence: scopes and owner, not only staff

Ownership evidence is the installed-app table (scopes + owner). A staff user list is a different record. Score apps, flag customer-data access, watch owner drift. Not a security attestation.

Shopify app scope, owner, and cost drift alerts

Drift alerts fire when installed-app scopes, owners, or costs change. Score the store, flag protected customer-data scopes, and track duplicate paid categories — without reading customer data.

Shopify collaborator access expired after 90 days — Reactivate

Shopify collaborator access expires after 90 days without login. That is not staff Suspend. Use Actions → Reactivate user, or Remove and a new request. Access Audit Guard does not Reactivate.

Shopify collaborator request code: stop open Partner requests

Shopify’s collaborator request code lives under Settings → Users → Security. Only Partners you share the code with can ask for access. Access Audit Guard does not rotate the code.

Staff access audit: permission drift is not a one-time user list

Staff access audit for Shopify. Admin user lists are a snapshot. Re-audit staff, collaborators, and app scopes, flag customer-data access, and export evidence. Not a security attestation.
