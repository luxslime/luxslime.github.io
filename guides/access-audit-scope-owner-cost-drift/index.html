Scope · owner · cost drift

Drift alerts fire when scopes, owners, or costs change — not only when you open a one-time app list

Staff Access Audit Guard (Shopify App Store) reviews installed app access, granted scopes, staff access records, and ownership evidence without reading customer or order data. Apps request scopes — including AI and agentic apps. The app classifies each granted scope, flags protected customer-data and broad-write access, and scores your store Low to High (higher is safer). Staff and collaborator reviews get overdue flags; drift alerts fire when scopes, owners, or costs change. Manual app-cost tracking flags duplicate paid categories. This page is not legal advice.

A snapshot list is not the same job as drift after install

Shopify’s admin shows which apps are installed today. Drift answers what changed after that: new or wider scopes, a different install owner, or cost moves — including duplicate paid categories you track manually — without opening customer or order records.

How to watch scope, owner, and cost drift

  1. Run the first audit on install and score the store Low to High (higher is safer).
  2. Classify granted scopes and flag protected customer-data and broad-write access.
  3. Keep ownership evidence so owner changes are visible.
  4. Treat drift alerts on scope, owner, or cost changes — and flag duplicate paid categories in manual app-cost tracking.
