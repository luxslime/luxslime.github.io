Textile label app comparison

FibreLedger vs Fibra: Textile Labels — Annex I fibre checks vs label authoring and care symbols

Direct answer. Choose Fibra: Textile Labels when you want to author EU fiber composition, ISO 3758-style care symbols, and responsible-person details in admin and display them on product pages, with a multilingual preview in EN, FR, DE, ES, and IT. Choose FibreLedger when you want every fibre checked against the official Annex I catalogue in EN, DE, and FR, trade names such as Lycra or Tencel flagged, and French places of manufacture recorded for product pages.
