AI shopping readiness app comparison

Agentic Catalog by momiji vs Agentic Catalog Repair — readiness score and crawler rules vs issue inbox and barcode fixes

Direct answer. Choose Agentic Catalog Repair when you want product and variant data scanned from the Admin, rendered product-page structured-data diagnostics reviewed in an issue inbox, and supported barcode or GTIN fixes applied with audit history and rollback. Choose Agentic Catalog by momiji when you want llms.txt and robots.txt rules checked for GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot, Product JSON-LD and Open Graph completeness audited on live product pages, and policy, shipping, tax, domain, and product data reviewed in one readiness score.
