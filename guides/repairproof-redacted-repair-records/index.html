Redacted repair records

A repair job needs a redacted record trail — not an inbox full of unredacted customer mail

RepairProof EU (Shopify App Store) verifies purchases before accepting repair requests, issues revision-linked repair forms with checksums, tracks diagnosis, quote, repair, return, and closure, and preserves redacted repair records from intake through return. This page is the redacted-record job — not the stage-tracking sibling. The app does not certify repairability or decide your legal obligations. This page is not legal advice.

Redaction is how repair evidence stays usable without leaking inbox contents

Loose email threads keep full customer messages forever. A redacted repair record keeps the stage trail from intake through return while stripping what you should not retain in the clear. That is separate from verifying the purchase and separate from moving a job across diagnosis → quote → repair → return → closure.

How to keep redacted repair records

  1. Verify the purchase before you accept the repair request.
  2. Issue revision-linked forms and move the job through diagnosis, quote, repair, return, and closure.
  3. Preserve the redacted repair record for each stage instead of forwarding unredacted mailbox threads.
  4. Retrieve the redacted trail when you need proof of what happened — not the original customer inbox.
