Redacted repair records

A repair job needs a redacted record trail — not an unredacted mailbox thread

Direct answer. A Shopify inbox or shared mailbox can store repair emails, but those threads stay unredacted and are not a stage-linked repair file from intake through return. RepairProof EU verifies purchases before accepting repair requests, issues revision-linked forms with checksums, tracks diagnosis through closure, and preserves redacted repair records from intake through return.

Redaction is how repair evidence stays usable without leaking inbox contents

Loose email threads keep full customer messages forever. A redacted repair record keeps the stage trail from intake through return while stripping what you should not retain in the clear.

How to keep redacted repair records

  1. Verify the purchase before you accept the repair request.
  2. Issue revision-linked forms and move the job through diagnosis, quote, repair, return, and closure.
  3. Preserve the redacted repair record for each stage instead of forwarding unredacted mailbox threads.
  4. Retrieve the redacted trail when you need proof of what happened — not the original customer inbox.
