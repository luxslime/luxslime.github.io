Deposit % on B2B checkout

A deposit % is not Net Terms and not a required PO number — three different checkout jobs

B2B Terms & PO Guard (Shopify App Store) is a Shopify app that validates purchase-order numbers by format, prefix, regex, length, and uniqueness, previews a PO rule against a sample number before you publish, sets deposit percentage guardrails, and routes oversized B2B orders for review. D2C and no-company checkouts stay untouched. It does not extend credit, collect card deposits as a payment service, or replace native company payment terms. PO rules and live previews work on every plan; checkout enforcement (PO, deposits, review) runs on Shopify Plus B2B. Confirm the live listing and Shopify B2B docs for your store. This guide does not add prices.

Three jobs: Net Terms, a required PO string, and a deposit %

Merchants searching “require 20% deposit on company checkout” often land on Downpay / Depo threads or Help Center payment terms. Those answers mix three checkout jobs. Keep them separate.

Three B2B checkout jobs that must not be mixed
Job What it controls What it does not do
A. Net Terms / company payment terms When the company pays. Shopify B2B (on Basic, Grow and Advanced since 2 April 2026, and on Plus) includes company catalogs and a PO number field; payment terms and order review are Plus features. Confirm Shopify Help — payment terms for your store. Does not validate a PO string. Does not, by itself, replace a listed deposit-% guardrail or order-review hold.
B. Required PO string Format, prefix, regex, length, and uniqueness so the PO box cannot be empty or junk. That job is the sibling require a PO number on B2B checkout. The listing app reads cart attribute po_guard_po_number, not cart.poNumber. Does not set when the invoice is due. Does not collect a percentage of this order.
C. Deposit % guardrail (this page) Require a percentage on the B2B order (listing: 1–99% style guardrails) and/or hold oversized orders for review. Reduces current-order risk. Does not replace credit limits across unpaid invoices. Does not underwrite the balance. Does not become Net Terms.

A deposit % and an order-review hold can sit next to a required PO. They do not replace it. Native Net Terms can sit next to both. Do not copy a postal PO Box rule onto any of these fields.

Native path: payment terms and draft-order invoices — not a universal 20% toggle

Use native company payment terms when the job is “this location pays on Net 30.” Shopify B2B (on Basic, Grow and Advanced since 2 April 2026, and on Plus) includes company catalogs and a PO number field; payment terms and order review are Plus features. Confirm Help Center — Setting up payment terms in B2B for the terms your store actually shows.

For offline or trade-show capture, Shopify documents draft-order invoices so you can send an invoice instead of taking a full storefront checkout on the floor. That is a capture path. It is not a storefront rule that every company checkout must leave a 20% deposit.

Help Center also documents deposit collection on some B2B surfaces. There is still no single no-code “20% deposit on every company checkout” toggle that replaces a validation app’s deposit-% guardrail and optional review hold. Do not invent one from this page. Confirm Help Center and the live listing for your store. PO rules and live previews work on every plan; checkout enforcement (PO, deposits, review) runs on Shopify Plus B2B.

App path: set the deposit guardrail, publish only what you mean, prove one company checkout

The listing job on this page is a deposit percentage guardrail (and optional order review) on company checkout — not a payment gateway and not native Net Terms.

  1. Open the B2B Terms & PO Guard listing (apps.shopify.com/b2b-terms-guard) and install it. The App Store title is B2B PO Guard — Require PO. That is the same published app, not a second listing.
  2. Set deposit percentage guardrails as the listing describes (1–99% style, per B2B order). Optionally set a review threshold so oversized orders wait for approval.
  3. If you also need a required PO string, set format / prefix / regex / length / uniqueness and preview a sample number before you publish. The app reads po_guard_po_number. Preview and publish only the rules you mean.
  4. Place one company test checkout that should hit the deposit or review rule you published. Confirm a D2C / no-company cart is untouched.
  5. Roll back if the live rule is wrong. Keep that first successful company checkout as the operating example before you widen locations.

The app applies the guardrails you publish. It does not collect the card deposit as a PSP, extend credit, or replace the company payment terms you already set in Admin. Confirm the live listing rather than this page.

What this page is not

  • Not the full required-PO guide. That job is B2B checkout can skip a purchase-order number unless a validation rule requires it.
  • Not a postal PO Box address block. That job is block PO Box on billing.
  • Not legal advice, credit insurance, or a chargeback guarantee.
  • Not “this guarantees the buyer pays the balance.” A deposit % reduces current-order risk. It does not close unpaid invoices across the account.
