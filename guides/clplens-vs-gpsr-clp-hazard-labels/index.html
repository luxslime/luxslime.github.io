CLP hazard app comparison

CLPLens vs GPSR & CLP Hazard Labels — hazard profiles and gap review vs multilingual CLP + GPSR blocks

Direct answer. Choose GPSR & CLP Hazard Labels when you want CLP hazard details and the GPSR responsible person with a full EU address on product pages, with Safety Data Sheet codes resolved to official EUR-Lex wording in 24 EU languages. Choose CLPLens when you want product hazard profiles with GHS pictograms and statements, synced to app-owned metafields for a theme block, plus a review of missing hazard fields before you publish product pages.
