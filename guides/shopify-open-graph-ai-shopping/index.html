Shopify Open Graph tags for AI shopping agents

Open Graph is not Product JSON-LD — both can be incomplete on a live Shopify PDP

Agentic Catalog by momiji / Agentic Catalog Readiness (Shopify App Store) is a Shopify app that scores readiness including llms.txt and robots.txt rules for GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot, plus live Product JSON-LD and Open Graph completeness on product pages, as listed. It does not rewrite theme Liquid, does not guarantee AI recommendations or ChatGPT placement, and does not replace counsel. Confirm live listing text rather than this page. This guide does not add prices or a plan matrix.

Job: stop treating share/unfurl Open Graph tags as the same surface as Product JSON-LD. Agents and unfurlers may read different markup. Completing one does not auto-fix the others.

llms.txt, Product JSON-LD, and Open Graph are three jobs

Crawler policy, Product JSON-LD, and Open Graph are different live surfaces
Surface What it is What completing it does not do
llms.txt / robots.txt Crawler policy and a public hint of which URLs an LLM-facing agent should prefer Put Product structured data or share meta into the PDP <head>
Product JSON-LD A structured Product graph in the page (often a JSON-LD script) Emit Open Graph share tags, or rewrite robots.txt
Open Graph Share / unfurl meta in <head> (property="og:…") Complete Product JSON-LD identifiers or offers

A theme can emit basic og:title / og:image on a product page while Product JSON-LD is still missing identifiers or offers. The reverse is also true. View both surfaces on the live URL.

Native theme path: View source, not a Facebook debugger alone

Many Online Store themes emit some product Open Graph tags from the product object. Shopify’s Liquid Open Graph tags example (HTTP 200) shows a social-meta-tags.liquid snippet that sets og:site_name, og:url, og:title, og:type, and og:description, and on product templates pulls title, type product, and product images from the product object. Shopify also documents that if a theme omits og:image, the platform can generate fallback og:image, og:image:secure_url, og:image:width, and og:image:height from the page_image object (HTTP 200). Theme SEO metadata for title, description, and canonical URLs is separate: Add SEO metadata to your theme.

Those are share / unfurl properties Shopify theme docs support. They are not a “required for ChatGPT” property checklist — Shopify’s primary sources do not publish one. Do not treat a third-party blog’s OG key list as what any app scores.

Open a live product page (and the homepage if you care about store-level share tags). Use View source. Search for og: in <head> and for application/ld+json Product markup. Facebook’s sharing debugger checks how Facebook unfurls a URL. It does not prove agent readiness, and it does not prove JSON-LD completeness.

JSON-LD is the sibling job: identifiers and offers live there

This page’s job is the OG vs JSON-LD confusion. Identifiers, offers, and the rest of the Product graph belong on the JSON-LD sibling: crawler rules and product JSON-LD. First-barcode / GTIN Admin notes are Shopify GTIN / barcode for AI shopping agents. Shopify’s Liquid structured_data filter is a JSON-LD path, not an Open Graph path. Completing share tags does not put a GTIN into JSON-LD.

Not the same as robots.txt, llms.txt, GTIN Admin, or a Merchant Center feed

  • robots.txt allow vs block for GPTBot, ClaudeBot, or PerplexityBot is crawler policy. That path is GPTBot / ClaudeBot / PerplexityBot allow vs block.
  • agents.md / llms.txt customization is a theme-template context file. That path is customize Shopify agents.md and llms.txt.
  • GTIN / first-barcode Admin fields are product identifiers. That path is the GTIN guide. Open Graph does not write Admin barcodes.
  • Google Merchant Center feed rows are a feed job. A feed fix does not, by itself, put Open Graph or Product JSON-LD on the live PDP.

Fix the matching surface. Do not assume one of these jobs rewrites the others.
