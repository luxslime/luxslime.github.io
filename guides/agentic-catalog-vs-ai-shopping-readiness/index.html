Agentic Catalog vs AI Shopping Readiness

Direct answer. To check whether AI crawlers and shopping agents can read a Shopify store, look at the robots.txt rules for named AI crawlers, the store's llms.txt, and Product JSON-LD and Open Graph on live product pages. Agentic Catalog by momiji checks llms.txt and robots.txt rules for GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot, audits Product JSON-LD and Open Graph completeness on live product pages, and reviews policy, shipping, tax, domain, and product data in one readiness score. AI Shopping Readiness fits when you want a read-only catalog clarity score and a product work queue.
