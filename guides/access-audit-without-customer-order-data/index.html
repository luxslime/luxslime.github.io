Access audit without customer data

Audit staff and app access without the audit app itself reading customer or order data

Direct answer. In Shopify Admin, open Settings → Users to review staff roles and permissions, and Settings → Apps and sales channels to open each install’s data access one by one — Admin has no store-wide access audit that reviews app scopes, staff records, and ownership evidence in one place. Staff Access Audit Guard reviews installed app access, granted scopes, staff access records, and ownership evidence without the audit app itself reading customer or order data.

Staff Access Audit Guard (Shopify App Store) reviews installed app access, granted scopes, staff access records, and ownership evidence without reading customer or order data; scores access risk; flags apps that can read or write customer data; and tracks staff and collaborator access with review reminders. This page is the audit-without-the-audit-app-reading-customer-or-order-data slice — not the customer-data scope list (which installed apps hold those scopes), ownership evidence alone, risk score, or 30/90-day reminders. This page is not legal advice.

The audit app should not need customer or order payloads

Opening a customer profile or order history is a different job from reviewing which apps and staff accounts hold access. Keep the audit on install metadata, staff records, and ownership evidence so the review itself does not pull customer or order payloads. Which installed apps hold customer-data scopes is the scopes sibling.

How to audit access without reading customer or order data

  1. Run a store-wide access review that reads install metadata and staff/collaborator access records — not customer or order payloads.
  2. Confirm the audit app path does not open customer profiles or order history.
  3. When you need which installs hold customer-data scopes, use the scopes sibling.
  4. Keep ownership evidence and risk scores alongside the review for internal follow-up.
