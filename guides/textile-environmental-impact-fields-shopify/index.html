Textile environmental-impact fields

Annex I fibre names are not environmental-impact fields — record impact information separately

Direct answer. Shopify Admin product fields do not give you a dedicated textile environmental-impact record next to fibre composition — you can paste impact copy into the description, but nothing structures environmental-impact information for textile listings the way Annex I names fibre composition. FibreLedger records environmental-impact information on textile products alongside Annex I fibre checks and French origin fields.

FibreLedger Textile Labels (Shopify App Store) checks every fibre name against the official Annex I catalogue in EN, DE and FR, flags trade names such as Lycra, records French origin and environmental-impact information, and retains EPR registration and audit records. The app does not decide legal compliance. This page is not legal advice.

Fibre composition and environmental-impact are different fields

Annex I names answer “what is the fibre called.” Environmental-impact information answers a different buyer and regulator question. Keeping impact copy only in the description mixes claims with composition and makes audit harder than structured fields.

Install FibreLedger Textile Labels, then record environmental-impact information on textile products.

How to record environmental-impact information on textile products

  1. Confirm fibre composition uses Annex I names (not trade names).
  2. Record environmental-impact information in the product impact fields.
  3. Add French places of manufacture when the listing targets France.
  4. Confirm the live product page shows composition and impact information as intended.

Not the same as Annex I names, France origin, or EPR audit records

  • Annex I fibre names — EU textile fibre composition.
  • France places of manufacture — France origin vs Annex I.
  • EPR registration / audit — textile EPR records.
  • Generic green claims — EU generic environmental claims ban.
