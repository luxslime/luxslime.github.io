When do EU right-to-repair proof records start to matter?

Directive (EU) 2024/1799 on common rules promoting the repair of goods is to be transposed and applied from 31 July 2026. Manufacturers of Annex II goods have an obligation to repair during the reparability window. Repairers may use the European Repair Information Form in Annex I. Confirm your Member State’s measures, not this page. This is not legal advice. Confirm Directive (EU) 2024/1799.

Is the European Repair Information Form mandatory for every request?

The form is voluntary for repairers. If you offer it, it has to be on a durable medium, before the consumer is bound, and the stated conditions stay valid for 30 calendar days (or longer if you agree). Accepting the form binds the repairer to those conditions. A loose email thread is not that record.

What belongs in a revision-linked repair proof file?

Purchase verification before intake, diagnosis and quote details, a revision-linked repair information form (with a checksum if you issue one), then repair, return, and closure steps. Product service profiles can hold indicative repair information, spare-parts notes, and support periods. Keep a redacted audit trail rather than an unbounded mailbox.

Does keeping forms mean I met the manufacturer obligation to repair?

No. The manufacturer obligation to repair Annex II goods, spare-part pricing, and anti-circumvention rules are legal duties. A request file documents the after-sales workflow you ran. It does not decide whether the product is in Annex II, whether a refusal was lawful, or whether the price was reasonable.

Is this the same as CRA duty records or an EUDR lot registry?

No. CRA files are cybersecurity reporting for products with digital elements. EUDR files map supplier lots to DDS reference numbers. Right-to-repair proof is after-sales service records for goods consumers want repaired. Do not mix those packs.

Does an app make after-sales “right-to-repair compliant”?

No. The app organizes merchant-supplied records. It does not determine legal compliance, guarantee repair outcomes, or provide legal advice. You still choose what to record and what to issue to the consumer.
