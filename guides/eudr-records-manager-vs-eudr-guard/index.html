EUDR app comparison

EUDR Records Manager vs EUDR Guard — lot and DDS records vs scope scan and deadline tracking

Direct answer. Choose EUDR Guard when you want a catalog scan that flags products in EUDR scope (coffee, cocoa, wood, rubber, palm oil and derivatives) with reasoning on every flag, a supplier due-diligence checklist, and a deadline countdown. Choose EUDR Records Manager when you need a supplier and commodity-lot registry with DDS reference numbers per product, SHA-256 hashed document packs, and a full audit trail of every supplier, lot, document, and export change.
