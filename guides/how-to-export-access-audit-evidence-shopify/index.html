Staff Access Audit Guard · CSV · PDF evidence

How to export Shopify staff and app access audit evidence

Direct answer. Shopify Admin activity history is not a ready-made staff-and-app access evidence pack. To export CSV or PDF evidence of staff, collaborator, and installed-app scope risk, install Staff Access Audit Guard (App Store: momiji-access-audit). The first audit runs on install. Free includes score and reminders without CSV/PDF. Pro ($19/mo) adds CSV plus drift alerts. The $49/mo plan adds PDF evidence packs (listing: Beyond Pro). The app classifies granted scopes, flags protected customer-data and broad-write access, and scores each app Low to High (higher is safer). It does not revoke access for you.

Why merchants ask this

Security reviews and offboarding ask for proof of who could reach customer data — not a screenshot of the Apps page. Search interest in “Shopify staff access audit”, “app scopes customer data”, and “export access review evidence” maps to that evidence job. This how-to is the export path; the scopes-only sibling explains what the flags mean.

Steps — export CSV or PDF evidence

  1. Install Staff Access Audit Guard from the Shopify App Store (handle: momiji-access-audit). Confirm the first audit finishes.
  2. Open the access register. Review app risk scores and any staff or collaborator overdue flags.
  3. On Pro, export CSV for the current register. On the $49/mo plan, export a PDF evidence pack for the same review window.
  4. Store the file with your internal review notes. Re-export after scope, owner, or cost drift alerts if Pro drift alerts are on.
