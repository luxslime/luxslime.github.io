Installed-app scope audit

Which installed apps can reach customer data? Read granted scopes — not the customer records

In Shopify admin, open Settings > Apps, select each installed app, and review its data access and permissions details to see whether it can reach customer data. To review every install at once, Staff Access Audit Guard classifies each granted scope, flags apps — including AI apps — that can read or write customer data, and scores the store Low to High (higher is safer).

Staff Access Audit Guard (Shopify App Store) reviews installed app access, granted scopes, staff access records, and ownership evidence without reading customer or order data. Apps request scopes — including AI and agentic apps. The app classifies each granted scope, flags protected customer-data and broad-write access, and scores your store Low to High (higher is safer). This page is not legal advice.

Scope classification is not the same as opening customer records

Shopify’s admin shows which apps are installed. A granted-scope audit answers a different question: which of those installs hold protected customer-data scopes or broad-write access, classified and scored, without the auditor reading customer or order data.

How to review app scopes for customer-data access

  1. Open the installed-app access view and read each app’s granted scopes.
  2. Flag protected customer-data scopes and broad-write access.
  3. Score the store Low to High (higher is safer) and note overdue staff or collaborator reviews.
  4. Keep ownership evidence for who owns each install when scopes or owners change.
