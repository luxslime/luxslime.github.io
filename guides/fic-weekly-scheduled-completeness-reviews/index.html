Weekly FIC completeness

A one-off allergen check is not a weekly FIC completeness review with an audit trail

Direct answer. Shopify Admin has no weekly scheduled review that re-checks food allergen, ingredient, nutrition, net quantity, origin, operator, and storage gaps and keeps an audit trail of every change — you can re-open products by hand, but nothing schedules that completeness pass. EU Food Info Guard runs weekly scheduled completeness reviews plus a full audit trail of every change.

EU Food Info Guard (Shopify App Store) reviews allergen, ingredient, nutrition, net quantity, origin, operator, and storage gaps before food product display, detects Annex II allergens from ingredient text, and runs weekly scheduled completeness reviews with an audit trail of every change. This page is the scheduled-review slice — not the allergen overview, declared-list mismatch, or storage-gap siblings. This page is not legal advice.

Scheduled completeness ≠ one product fix

Fixing one allergen line today does not re-check the catalog next week. Weekly completeness reviews catch drift after edits; the audit trail shows what changed.

How to run weekly FIC completeness reviews

  1. Install EU Food Info Guard and connect the food catalog you sell online.
  2. Confirm allergen, storage, nutrition, and operator fields are in scope for review.
  3. Enable weekly scheduled completeness reviews.
  4. Read the audit trail after each run and close new gaps before the next publish cycle.
