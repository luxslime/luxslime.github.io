Staff access audit app comparison

Best staff access audit app for Shopify: app-scope and staff reviews vs admin change history

Direct answer. Choose Adminlog ‑ Audit Trail when you want a searchable history of what changed in admin — products, inventory, orders, and refunds — with old and new values, undo for product changes, and alerts for unusual activity. Choose Staff Access Audit Guard when you want every installed app’s granted scopes classified and scored, customer-data and broad-write access flagged, and staff and collaborator access tracked with 30/90-day review reminders, without reading customer or order data. This page is not a ranking and not legal advice.

Staff Access Audit Guard (Shopify App Store) reviews installed app access, granted scopes, staff access records, and ownership evidence without reading customer or order data. Adminlog ‑ Audit Trail (Shopify App Store) records admin changes by staff, collaborators, and other apps so you can see who changed what and restore a previous product version. Re-open both listings before you decide. Native Shopify still shows staff under Users and app permissions under Settings > Apps; Shopify’s Help Center page on app personal information documents that uninstalling an app revokes its access to data.

Free check first: App scope access reader runs in your browser and sends nothing. Paste an app’s access scopes and see which touch customer data or can write. It is a reading aid for a review conversation, not a score and not what any app checks.

Native Shopify is still the user list and the app permission screen

Shopify Admin already lists staff and collaborators, and Settings > Apps shows each install. Since 11 March 2026 Shopify also shows Admin API activity, data-privacy notes, and app-permission history on that screen. Neither app replaces those screens. Adminlog adds a change log on top of admin edits. Staff Access Audit Guard adds a store-wide scope classification, a Low-to-High score, and review reminders. Confirm the live Help Center and changelog; this page is not a security attestation.

How to choose

If a price, description, or stock level was changed and you need old and new values, who did it (where Shopify provides it), and one-click restore for product changes, Adminlog ‑ Audit Trail fits that job. If you need every installed app’s scopes classified, customer-data and broad-write access flagged, staff and collaborator reviews with 30/90-day reminders, and evidence exports, without the audit app reading customer or order data, Staff Access Audit Guard fits that job. Many stores need both jobs; they are not substitutes. Open Adminlog ‑ Audit Trail and Staff Access Audit Guard and re-read the live copy.
