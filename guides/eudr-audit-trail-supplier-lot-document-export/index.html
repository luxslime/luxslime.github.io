EUDR audit trail

Admin activity history is not an EUDR audit trail of every supplier, lot, document, and export change

Direct answer. Shopify Admin records staff activity history, but that is not a full EUDR audit trail of every supplier, lot, document, and export change. EUDR Records Manager keeps a full audit trail of every supplier, lot, document, and export change.

EUDR Records Manager (Shopify App Store) keeps a supplier and commodity-lot registry with DDS reference numbers, runs weekly gap review for missing supplier, country, or DDS fields, builds SHA-256 hashed document packs, and keeps a full audit trail of every supplier, lot, document, and export change. This page is the full audit-trail slice — not the DDS lot registry, weekly gap review, or hashed document packs alone. This page is not legal advice.

Why a full change trail matters for EUDR records

When a supplier, lot, document, or export changes, reviewers need to see what moved and when — not only the latest file in Admin activity history.

Install EUDR Records Manager

How to keep an EUDR audit trail of supplier, lot, document, and export changes

  1. Open the audit trail after a supplier, lot, document, or export record changes.
  2. Review what moved and when on that trail.
  3. Do not treat Admin activity history as the EUDR change trail.
  4. Keep the DDS lot registry, weekly gap review, and SHA-256 hashed packs on their sibling pages.
