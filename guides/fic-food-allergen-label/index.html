Food allergen labelling has to appear before purchase, not only on delivery

Job: show EU FIC allergen information on the product page before checkout — the Annex II list, including any allergen the ingredient text mentions but the declared list omits. A sticker on the parcel is too late for distance selling.

This page is the pre-purchase allergen job, not a listing mirror and not legal advice. Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011 is the food-information rule. UK PPDS is a neighbouring regime, not a substitute. Confirm the live text before you ship copy.

Does EU FIC require allergen labelling before purchase in distance selling?

Mandatory food information — including substances or products causing allergies or intolerances in Annex II — has to be available before the purchase is concluded for distance selling, and still appear at delivery. Nutrition and origin sit in the same pre-purchase set. This page is not legal advice. Confirm Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011, especially Articles 9 and 14, not this paraphrase.

What are the 14 Annex II allergens?

Annex II lists cereals containing gluten, crustaceans, eggs, fish, peanuts, soybeans, milk, nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphur dioxide and sulphites above the stated threshold, lupin, and molluscs, plus products thereof, with the exceptions written in that annex. Use the live annex, not a nickname list from this page.

What if ingredient text mentions an allergen the declared list omits?

That mismatch is the detection job: flag the allergen the ingredients mention but the declared list does not. Fix the declared list (or the ingredient text) before you leave the product page live. Detection is a hint from text, not a lab assay.

Can I put allergens only in a delivery note or on the parcel?

No for the pre-purchase duty. Delivery-only notes are a different surface. Distance selling still has to show the mandatory particulars before checkout. Keep delivery notes apart from the product-page facts.

Did honey origin rules change in 2026?

Honey origin labelling rules changed 14 June 2026. Origin remains a pre-purchase fact alongside allergens and nutrition when it is required. This page’s main job is allergen labelling; origin is the neighbouring field on the same product page. Confirm the live honey-origin text, not this page.

Does an app make the product page FIC compliant?

No. The app organizes merchant data, detects Annex II names from ingredient text, and can sync a theme block. It does not determine legal compliance, calculate recipes, or print official labels. Not legal advice.
