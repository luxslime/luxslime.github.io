CLP hazard pictograms

Annex V assigns GHS01 to GHS09. The symbol names are exploding bomb through environment

Direct answer. In the consolidated text of Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008, CELEX 02008R1272-20260701 (EN, 01.07.2026; reference line 02008R1272 — EN — 01.07.2026 — 031.001), Annex V is titled “HAZARD PICTOGRAMS.” It assigns GHS01 to the symbol exploding bomb, GHS02 to flame, GHS03 to flame over circle, GHS04 to gas cylinder, GHS05 to corrosion, GHS06 to skull and crossbones, GHS07 to exclamation mark, GHS08 to health hazard, and GHS09 to environment. GHS05 and GHS07 each appear in more than one part of the annex. The hazard classes printed beside each code are in the table below. The consolidation shows pictogram specimens. This page does not reproduce them. This page is not legal advice.

Checklist before you copy a code

  • Open Annex V on the 1 July 2026 consolidated text. The symbol names and classes below are that English text.
  • The introduction says the pictograms shall satisfy this Annex and Annex I, section 1.2, and conform in symbols and general format to the specimens shown. This page does not embed those specimens.
  • Minimum size is CLP pictogram size. This page does not repeat the dimensions.
  • The words that go with a code are Annex III hazard statements. Signal words are Danger and Warning.
  • The label set that includes pictograms is Article 17. Showing them on a product page is the product-page job.

What the introduction says

Annex V’s introduction says: “The hazard pictograms for each hazard class, differentiation of a hazard class and hazard category shall satisfy the provisions of this Annex and Annex I, section 1.2 and conform in terms of symbols and general format, to the specimens shown.”

Annex V prints “Self reactive” without a hyphen for GHS01, section 2.8, and “Self-reactive” with a hyphen for GHS02, section 2.8. It prints “Oxidising” and “Desensitised” in the English text. Section 2.2’s flammable-gas line ends with a full stop after “1B” in the consolidation.

Classes Annex V says do not get a pictogram

Part 1, section 1.6, says a pictogram is not required for: Section 2.1, Explosives of Division 1.5; Section 2.1, Explosives of Division 1.6; Section 2.2, Flammable gases, hazard Category 2; Section 2.3, Aerosols, hazard Category 3; Section 2.8, Self-reactive substances and mixtures, Type G; Section 2.15, Organic peroxides, Type G.

Part 2, section 2.5, says a pictogram is not required for Section 3.7, Reproductive toxicity, Effects on or via lactation, additional hazard category.

Part 3 says a pictogram is not required for Section 4.1, Hazardous to the aquatic environment — Long-term hazard categories: Chronic 3, Chronic 4.
