Prop 65 cited templates

Publish Prop 65 warnings from cited 27 CCR templates — not freehand theme paste

Prop 65 Warning Manager (Shopify App Store) displays California Prop 65 warnings on product pages and keeps an audit trail of warning edits, assignments, and storefront activation events. Every warning needs merchant approval before it goes live. Publish from templates citing 27 CCR §§ 25601–25607, including 2028 short-form text with chemical names. The app does not decide whether an exposure requires a warning. This page is not legal advice.

A cited template is a different job from typing warning text into the theme

Theme paste and a one-off Product Disclosure can put any string on the page. A cited template starts from wording tied to 27 CCR §§ 25601–25607 — including 2028 short-form text with chemical names when that is the content you rely on — then requires merchant approval before go-live, with picks, edits, and activations timestamped in the audit trail.

How to publish from a cited template

  1. Choose a warning template that cites 27 CCR §§ 25601–25607 (including 2028 short-form with chemical names when that applies).
  2. Assign it to the products that need it.
  3. Require merchant approval before any warning goes live.
  4. Keep the audit trail of warning edits, assignments, and storefront activation events.
