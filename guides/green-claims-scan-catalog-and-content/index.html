Green claims catalog scan

Scanning the catalog for green-claim wording is not the same job as keeping dated evidence

Direct answer. Shopify Admin lets you edit product descriptions by hand, but nothing scans the catalog and content for green-claim wording in one pass. Green Claims Guard scans green marketing wording in catalog and content so you can review flags before you publish cleaned copy — evidence vault and cited-provision detail are separate follow-on jobs.

Green Claims Guard (Shopify App Store) scans green marketing wording in catalog and content, flags green-claim wording and names the exact provision it cites, and exports dated evidence records for review. It does not decide whether a claim is lawful. This page is the catalog-and-content scan slice — not cited-provision detail alone, evidence vault, recycled %, compostable wording, UWG DE, or the generic ban. This page is not legal advice.

Why scan catalog and content first

Hand edits miss repeated eco adjectives across collections. A catalog-and-content scan surfaces wording to review before you argue provisions or file evidence.

How to scan catalog and content for green claims

  1. Run a scan across product catalog copy and related content fields you use for marketing claims.
  2. Review each flag and clean or substantiate the wording.
  3. When you need the rule name on a flag, use the cited-provision view.
  4. Keep dated evidence when a claim must be substantiated later.
