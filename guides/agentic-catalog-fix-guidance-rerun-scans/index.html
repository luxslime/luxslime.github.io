Fix guidance + rerun

A readiness score is not enough — review fix guidance, then rerun the scan

Direct answer. Shopify Admin has no single AI shopping readiness score with fix guidance you can act on and then rerun — you can edit robots.txt, theme JSON-LD, or policy pages by hand, but nothing ties gaps to a readiness checklist and a rescan. Agentic Catalog reviews fix guidance after a readiness scan and lets you rerun scans so the score reflects what you changed.

Agentic Catalog by momiji (Shopify App Store) checks llms.txt and robots.txt rules for GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot, audits Product JSON-LD and Open Graph completeness on live product pages, and reviews policy, shipping, tax, domain, and product data in one readiness score. After a scan, review fix guidance, rerun scans, and confirm the score moved. The app does not promise ranking or inclusion in any AI shopping surface. This page is not legal advice.

Score → guidance → rerun is the fix loop

Reading a score without guidance leaves gaps unprioritized. Guidance without a rerun leaves you unsure whether the change landed. This page is that loop — not the crawler/JSON-LD overview, not the policy/shipping/tax/domain slice, and not scheduled alerts.

How to review fix guidance and rerun scans

  1. Run a catalog readiness scan and open the flagged gaps.
  2. Review the fix guidance for each gap (crawler rules, live product markup, or policy / shipping / tax / domain).
  3. Apply the change on the storefront or Admin surface the guidance names.
  4. Rerun the scan and confirm the readiness score reflects the fix.

Not the same as readiness overview, policy slice, or scheduled alerts

  • Readiness overview — crawler rules and product JSON-LD.
  • Policy / shipping / tax / domain — same score, that slice.
  • Scheduled scans + alerts — daily or weekly scans with score-change alerts.
  • Open Graph ≠ Product JSON-LD — Open Graph is not Product JSON-LD.
