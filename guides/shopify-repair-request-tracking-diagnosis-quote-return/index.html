Track repair requests after you accept them

A repair request is five stages, not one email thread — record diagnosis and quote before work starts, and close the job with the return

RepairProof EU (Shopify App Store) is a Shopify app that verifies purchases before accepting repair requests, issues revision-linked repair forms with checksums, tracks diagnosis, quote, repair, return, and closure, and preserves redacted repair records from intake through return. After you accept a request, keep a stage record: diagnosis notes, the quote the customer accepted, the repair done, the return shipment, and closure. It does not determine legal compliance, guarantee outcomes, or provide legal advice.

Job: after intake, run the five listed stages — diagnosis, quote, repair, return, and closure — as merchant-owned facts on the request, not as a loose inbox thread. Purchase verification is the gate before stage 1. Revision-linked forms are the proof file, not this page.

Why not orders and returns alone

Shopify Help Center Returns and exchanges says you can manage returns and exchanges from the Orders page. Returns usually involve a refund, a return (receive an item back, with optional return shipping labels and tracking), and an exchange.

Processing and managing returns is the next step on that same path: after you receive and inspect the items, you process the return, issue a refund or collect payment, fulfill any exchange items, and close the return.

A repair job still needs its own stage record — diagnosis notes, the accepted quote, the repair done, the return shipment, and closure. Native returns document refund and exchange. They are not that five-stage file.

Where Directive (EU) 2024/1799 fits

Directive (EU) 2024/1799 on common rules promoting the repair of goods applies from 31 July 2026 after national transposition. Two articles matter at this altitude; this page is not legal advice.

Article 5 — obligation to repair. Member States shall ensure that, upon the consumer’s request, the manufacturer shall repair goods for which, and to the extent that, repairability requirements are provided for by Union legal acts listed in Annex II. That duty sits on manufacturers of those listed product groups — or, if the manufacturer is outside the Union, the authorised representative, importer, or distributor in that order — not on every retailer.

Article 4 — European Repair Information Form. Repairers may provide the consumer with the European Repair Information Form set out in Annex I. It is optional for repairers. If offered, it is provided on a durable medium before the consumer is bound, and the stated conditions stay valid for at least 30 calendar days. Form detail lives on the revision-linked proof guide.

Confirm the live directive and your Member State’s measures. A stage record documents the workflow you ran. It does not decide whether a unit is in Annex II or whether a refusal was lawful.

Before stage 1: verify the purchase

Do not open diagnosis on an unmatched inbox claim. Match the requester to a Shopify order and product, then accept. That intake gate is the sibling Verify the purchase before you accept a repair request. This page starts after that accept.

App path

  1. Open the RepairProof EU listing (apps.shopify.com/repairproof-eu) and install it on the store that takes repair requests.
  2. Verify purchases before accepting repair requests — the intake gate on the sibling page.
  3. Track diagnosis, quote, repair, return, and closure. Record diagnosis notes and the accepted quote before work starts. Close the job with the return.
  4. Issue revision-linked repair forms with checksums when you issue a form. Keep redacted repair records from intake through return.
