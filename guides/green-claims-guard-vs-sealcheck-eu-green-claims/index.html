Green claims app comparison

Green Claims Guard vs Sealcheck: EU Green Claims — cited provisions and evidence records vs theme-badge scans

Direct answer. Choose Sealcheck when you want products, collections, pages, blog articles, and your live theme scanned, including self-made sustainability badges, with every finding quoting the exact text and fixes applied only after you confirm them one phrase at a time. Choose Green Claims Guard when you want green marketing wording in your catalog and content flagged with the exact provision it cites, plus dated evidence records you can export for review.
