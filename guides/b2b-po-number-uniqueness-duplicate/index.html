B2B PO uniqueness

Native Net Terms do not stop a reused PO number — uniqueness is a separate checkout rule

Shopify's native Net Terms do not reject a PO number that was already used; you need a checkout validation rule that checks the PO number against previously used numbers and blocks the company checkout when it matches. B2B Terms & PO Guard validates PO numbers by format, prefix, regex, length, and uniqueness, and previews any PO rule live against a sample number before you publish.

B2B Terms & PO Guard (Shopify App Store) validates purchase-order numbers by format, prefix, regex, length, and uniqueness, previews a PO rule against a sample number before you publish, sets deposit percentage guardrails, and routes oversized B2B orders for review. It works alongside native Net Terms. D2C and no-company checkouts stay untouched. Checkout enforcement runs on Plus B2B; PO rules work on every plan. This page is not legal advice.

Uniqueness is not the same job as “PO required” or Net Terms

Native company payment terms control when the company pays. A required-PO rule rejects empty or malformed strings. Uniqueness rejects a number the buyer has already used — format/prefix/regex/length can still pass while a duplicate slips through unless uniqueness is part of the published rule. Preview a sample number before you publish.

How to add uniqueness to the PO rule

  1. Draft the PO rule with format, prefix, regex, length, and uniqueness as you need them.
  2. Preview the rule live against a sample number (including a known reuse) before publish.
  3. Publish for company checkout; D2C and no-company checkouts stay untouched.
  4. Keep Net Terms, deposit %, and order-review holds as separate jobs when you need them.
