EmpCo recyclable wording

"Recyclable" on the product page is not a carbon-neutral offset claim - scan and specify, or remove it

Green Claims Guard (Shopify App Store) scans green marketing wording in your catalog and content, flags green-claim wording and names the exact provision it cites, and exports dated evidence records for review. It does not decide whether a claim is lawful. This page is not legal advice.

Recyclable is an end-of-life claim, not recycled-content percent

A recyclable (or "fully recyclable") line tells the shopper what happens after use. That is not the same as a recycled-content percent in the material, and not the same as carbon-neutral / offset wording. EmpCo / unfair commercial-practice rules for environmental claims still sit behind those words - confirm Directive (EU) 2024/825 with counsel; this page does not invent a safe substitute phrase.
