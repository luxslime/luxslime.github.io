Prop 65 warning assignment

A Prop 65 warning assignment answers which products carry a warning — not only the wording on one page

To see which products carry a Prop 65 warning in Shopify's native Product Disclosures, turn on "Filter on the product list and in the Admin API" for the Disclosures metafield definition (Settings > Metafields and metaobjects > Products), then filter Products by the Prop 65 disclosure type and save the view. If you manage warnings in Prop 65 Warning Manager instead, every warning needs merchant approval before it goes live, so an assigned warning does not reach the storefront until you approve it.

Prop 65 Warning Manager (Shopify App Store) displays California Prop 65 warnings on product pages and keeps an audit trail of warning edits, assignments, and storefront activation events. Every warning needs merchant approval before it goes live. Publish from templates citing 27 CCR §§ 25601–25607, including 2028 short-form text with chemical names. The app does not decide whether an exposure requires a warning. This page is not legal advice.

Assignment is the product link — separate from activation and from theme paste

Theme paste and native Product Disclosures can put warning text on a single product page. A warning assignment is the catalog link: which products are tied to which warning, with merchant approval before go-live, and with those assignment events recorded in the audit trail of edits, assignments, and storefront activation events.

How to assign warnings across the catalog

  1. Choose a cited warning template (including 2028 short-form with chemical names when that is the content you rely on).
  2. Assign the warning to the products that need it — not only the one page you are editing by hand.
  3. Require merchant approval before any warning goes live.
  4. Keep the audit trail of warning edits, assignments, and storefront activation events.
