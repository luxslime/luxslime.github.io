Net 30 vs net 60

In Shopify B2B, net 30 and net 60 are Net (period) terms that start the day the order is placed

Direct answer. In trade invoices, “net 30” and “net 60” are often used to mean payment is due within 30 or 60 days. This page does not quote a bank or card-network rule for that wording. In Shopify B2B, payment terms are a store setting. Setting up payment terms in B2B says: “Payment terms define how long a company has to pay for an order. You can set payment terms for each company location.” The Net (period) row lists net 7, net 15, net 30, net 45, net 60, and net 90, all starting from the day the order is placed, and gives a net 30 example. Quotes below are from that Help page, checked 12 October 2026.

B2B PO Guard (Shopify App Store): “Validate PO numbers, deposits, Net terms, order review, and D2C no-op behavior for Shopify native B2B checkout workflows.” It does not set Shopify’s native payment terms.

Checklist

  • Read the Net (period) row before you treat net 30 and net 60 as different kinds of term. They are two periods on the same row.
  • Use the page’s clock: all of those terms start from the day the order is placed. The worked example is net 30.
  • Set the term on the company location, or on an individual draft order, as the table’s “Can be set on” column states.
  • Do not expect Shopify to capture the payment when the term expires. The sentence is quoted below.

The payment term types, as written

A note above the table: “If there’s a deposit requirement, then payment for the deposit is due up front. The remaining balance on the order is due on payment terms.”

Payment term types, from Shopify Help. The deposit note above the table is separate from these rows.
Payment term Description, as written Can be set on
No payment terms Payment is due immediately. This is the default setting. Company location and individual draft orders.
Net (period) Payment is due within a specific time period that you select. The available terms are net 7, net 15, net 30, net 45, net 60, and net 90. All terms start from the day the order is placed. For example, if you select net 30 terms, then customers need to pay for their orders within 30 days. Company location and individual draft orders.
Due on fulfillment Payment is due after all items in the order are fulfilled. Company location and individual draft orders.
Fixed date Payment is due on a specific calendar date in either the past or future. For example, payment is due on January 27, 2026. Individual draft orders.

Fixed date is draft-order only, as that last cell states. The how-to for choosing a term on a company location is Shopify B2B payment terms.

“2/10 net 30”

Outside Shopify, the phrase “2/10 net 30” is often used in trade invoices for an early-payment discount. This page does not quote an official definition of that phrase. Shopify’s Net (period) list above does not include that discount syntax.
