How is App Store Release & Review Benchmark different from App Review Regression & Release Report and App Release Review Regression Report?

App Store Release & Review Benchmark (taroyamada/app-release-category-review-benchmark-report): Benchmark Apple App Store and Google Play releases, ratings, and review regressions. Deliver source-linked app comparisons, alerts, reports, and optional exports. Not a single-app release-regression monitor (use App Review Regression & Release Report). Not an ASO keyword/review scrape of listings you already know. You supply Apple App IDs and/or Google Play IDs. App Review Regression & Release Report is for One-app release vs review regression (input app IDs; Store listing + reviews; Regression report). Not a multi-app category benchmark pack. App Release Review Regression Report is for Release-linked review regression report (input app IDs; Store reviews; Regression report). Not a category benchmark export. App Store & Google Play Reviews & ASO Scraper is for Reviews and ASO fields (input app IDs; Store listing + reviews; Review/ASO rows). Not a category benchmark report.
