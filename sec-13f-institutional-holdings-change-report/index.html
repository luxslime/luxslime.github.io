How is SEC 13F Institutional Holdings Change Report different from SEC EDGAR Scraper and SEC Insider Transaction Scraper?

SEC 13F Institutional Holdings Change Report (taroyamada/sec-13f-institutional-holdings-change-report): Track institutional 13F holdings using official SEC EDGAR filings. Receive source-linked holding-change alerts, reports, and optional exports. Not a general EDGAR 8-K/S-1 monitor. Not Form 4 insider transactions. Not Schedule 13D/13G beneficial-ownership. You supply filer watchlists. Live schema requires userAgent. SEC EDGAR Scraper is for 8-K and S-1 filing alerts (input tickers / CIKs; EDGAR; Filing rows). Not 13F holdings tables. SEC Insider Transaction Scraper is for Form 4 insider trades (input tickers / CIKs; EDGAR Form 4; Transaction rows). Not 13F institutional holdings. SEC 13D & 13G Beneficial Ownership Change Report is for Schedule 13D/13G (input watchlists + userAgent; EDGAR 13D/13G; Ownership-change alerts). Not 13F table holdings.
