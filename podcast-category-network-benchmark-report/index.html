How is Apple Podcasts Category Benchmark different from Apple Podcasts Chart Scraper and Apple Podcasts Scraper?

Apple Podcasts Category Benchmark (taroyamada/podcast-category-network-benchmark-report): Benchmark Apple Podcasts shows from RSS feeds, show URLs, or search terms you supply. Deliver source-linked show rows, network signals, category reports, and optional exports. Not an iTunes Search/charts dump (use Apple Podcasts Scraper / Chart Scraper). Not a media-buyer shortlist of sponsor-fit shows. You supply feeds or show URLs. Apple Podcasts Chart Scraper is for iTunes chart ranks (input country + chart; iTunes RSS charts; Rank rows). Not an RSS-fed category benchmark. Apple Podcasts Scraper is for Search, episodes, charts, reviews (input term / IDs; iTunes Search/Lookup; Show/episode rows). Not a category network benchmark report. Podcast Media Buyer Shortlist Scraper is for Sponsor-fit shortlist (input feeds / keywords; RSS episode text; Shortlist rows). Not a category benchmark report pack.
