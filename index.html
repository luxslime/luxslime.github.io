Guides

Shopify challenge pages and Apify first-run / Apple Podcasts guides. Wording on each guide is unchanged; this is the front-door index.

Shopify challenges

Staff permission drift after the last review is not caught by a one-time user list

B2B checkout can skip a purchase-order number unless a validation rule requires it

A deposit % is not Net Terms and not a required PO number — three different checkout jobs

AI shopping agents skip catalogs without crawler rules and complete product JSON-LD

Shopify already serves /agents.md — customize it with agents.md.liquid, not an app proxy

If robots.txt disallows AI crawlers, agents.md cannot save AI shopping visibility

GTIN is an AI-catalog input — put the GS1 barcode first when you store more than one

CLP hazard pictograms belong on the product page, not only on the parcel

UFI on the product page: part of the Art. 17 offer block from 1 Jan 2028 — not a substitute for the pack label

EU textile fibre composition must use Annex I names, not trade names

Fibre composition must be in the official language(s) of the Member State — Annex I names alone are not enough

France: show places of manufacture next to the price on Shopify (Code de l'environnement L. 541-9-1-2)

Food allergen labelling has to appear before purchase, not only on delivery

EU distance selling: nutrition declaration and net quantity belong on the product page, not only the parcel

EU honey origin: list countries (and blend %) on the product page before purchase — not only on the jar

Cyber Resilience Act Article 14 duties need a product file before an incident

Show the CRA support-period end date on the Shopify product page at purchase — the duty file is not the offer

EU right-to-repair proof needs revision-linked forms, not a loose email thread

EmpCo on the product page: show the repairability score when it exists — otherwise producer-supplied spare-parts facts, not invented copy

Supplier lots need an EUDR DDS reference number on file before 30 December 2026

Generic environmental claims on product copy are banned in the EU from 27 September 2026

“Carbon neutral” on the product page: EmpCo bans offset-based neutrality — not the same job as “eco-friendly”

Prop 65 short-form warnings need a chemical name on 2028 product pages

Block PO Box on the billing address, not just shipping

Leftover Admin access: leavers with 2FA, collaborators vs the bill, preview vs publish

Cannot suspend a Shopify collaborator — use Remove collaborator account, not staff Suspend

90-day collaborator expiry is not Suspend — use Reactivate (or Remove + a new request)

Block PO boxes: native shipping-rate hide vs checkout validation vs AddressFence

Require a street number at Shopify checkout after the Address Blocker sunset

Prop 65: native product disclosures vs an approval and audit-log app

Prop 65 on Shopify: California-only is a jurisdiction job, not an IP pop-up

Ship-to-California is a checkout job — US-CA on the disclosure is not the same control

Alcoholic beverages use the Prop 65 alcohol disclosure type — not the chemical cancer / reproductive types

Collaborator request code: only Partners you share the code with can ask for access

EUDR: who is in scope on 30 December 2026 vs mid-2027, and what records to keep

Stop retyping GPSR manufacturer and responsible-person fields on every product
