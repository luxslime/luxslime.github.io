How is SEC EDGAR Disclosure Candidate Watch different from SEC EDGAR Scraper and SEC Material Contract Intelligence?

SEC EDGAR Disclosure Candidate Watch (taroyamada/sec-edgar-material-disclosure-watch-scraper): This Actor watches SEC EDGAR public submissions for source-linked disclosure candidates, 8-K item metadata, filing deltas, exhibit links, and portfolio reports with source-linked context only. Not a full EDGAR filing-index monitor (use SEC EDGAR Scraper). Not Forms 3/4/5 insider rows. Not exhibit-contract extraction (use SEC Material Contract Intelligence). Informational only. Not investment advice. The $0.01 SEC filing-detected event is a live primary PPE key, not a stale actor-start. SEC EDGAR Scraper is for 8-K and S-1 filing alerts (input companies; Public EDGAR; Filing-index rows). Not Form D notices or 8-K item candidates. SEC Material Contract Intelligence is for Exhibit/contract leads (input issuers; EDGAR exhibits; Contract intelligence rows). Not Form D or insider Forms 3/4/5. SEC Insider Transaction Scraper is for Forms 3, 4, 5 (input issuers; EDGAR ownership forms; Insider transaction rows). Not Form D exempt offerings.

What input is required?

Live required fields: none listed (see live fields). Published exampleRunInput is shown below.

Field Type Default Notes
issuers string[] ["MSFT"] Issuers. Ticker symbols or CIK numbers to inspect.
formTypes string[] ["8-K", "10-K", "10-Q", "S-1"] Form types. SEC form types to include.
daysBack integer 90 Days back. How far back to inspect recent submissions. minimum=1 maximum=3650
disclosureKeywords string[] ["customer agreement", "acquisition"] Disclosure keywords. Source-text keywords that mark a disclosure candidate. The actor does not classify filing importance.
secUserAgent string taroyamada-sec-disclosure-watch/1.0 ops@example.com SEC User-Agent. SEC-compliant User-Agent with app and contact information. Example: your-company-edgar-watch/1.0 ops@yourcompany.com
generateReport boolean true Generate report. Emit a portfolio-level report row when filings are found.
emitUnchanged boolean false Emit unchanged rows. When false, previously seen filings are suppressed.
dryRun boolean false Dry run. When true, emit sample rows locally without external live calls or billing.
