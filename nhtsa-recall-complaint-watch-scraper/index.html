How is NHTSA Recall & Complaint Watch different from NHTSA Recall Scraper and Product Safety Recall Scraper?

NHTSA Recall & Complaint Watch (taroyamada/nhtsa-recall-complaint-watch-scraper): This Actor tracks official NHTSA recall and complaint records by make, model, model year, campaign, and component watch terms. Not the NHTSA digest Actor (taroyamada/nhtsa-vehicle-recall-digest). Not CPSC product recalls. Not FDA warning letters. Informational only. Not safety or legal advice. NHTSA Recall Scraper is for NHTSA recall digests (input vehicle watches; NHTSA public APIs; Recall digest rows). Not the alert/digest/report watch Actor. Product Safety Recall Scraper is for FDA/CPSC recall digests (input feeds; FDA/CPSC recall feeds; Recall digest rows). Not a CPSC marketplace watch with alert/report PPE. FDA Warning Letters Scraper is for FDA warning-letter digests (input feeds; FDA warning-letter feeds; Warning-letter digest rows). Not CPSC/NHTSA/EPA records.
