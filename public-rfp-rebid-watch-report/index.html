How is Public RFP Rebid Watch Report different from TED, SAM.gov & Grants Bid Alerts and Government Contract Award Change Report?

Public RFP Rebid Watch Report (taroyamada/public-rfp-rebid-watch-report): This Actor turns public RFP notices, award pages, SAM.gov/USAspending/Grants.gov links, or pasted public text into decision-ready RFP snapshot and rebid timing reports. Not a live TED/SAM opportunity search desk (use TED, SAM.gov & Grants Bid Alerts). Not USASpending award-change monitoring (use Government Contract Award Change Report). Informational only. Not a bid/no-bid decision. TED, SAM.gov & Grants Bid Alerts is for Open opportunities / tenders (input keywords / jurisdictions; SAM/TED/Grants notices; Opportunity rows). Not vendor-pursuit profiles, Form D notices, or post-award spend reports. Government Contract Award Change Report is for Post-award spend/award changes (input feeds; TED / USASpending awards; Award change rows). Not open bid queues. Grants.gov Scraper is for Grants.gov-only funding notices (input feeds / watch terms; Grants.gov; Funding digest rows). Not a multi-source TED/SAM desk.

How do dataset, webhook, and dry-run delivery work?

delivery defaults to dataset on the live schema. Dataset output is the billable surface when rows are written. webhookUrl is used when delivery is webhook (and typically not during dryRun). dryRun true validates or samples without the usual dataset/webhook side effects described on the Store schema. Unchanged runs that write zero default-dataset rows typically charge $0.00 on current PPE.
