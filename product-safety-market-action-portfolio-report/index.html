What input is required?

Live required fields: watchlists, monitorKey, initialRunMode, generateReport, emitRawRows, emitUnchanged, maxChargeUsd, dryRun. watchlists[] live properties: id, agency (CPSC, NHTSA, FDA), productTypes (FDA: drug, device, or food; max 3), productNames (max 20), keywords (max 20), brands (max 20), classifications (max 5), statuses (max 5), lookbackDays (min 1, max 3650), maxResults (min 1, max 100). Array minItems 1, maxItems 100. Default watchlist is battery CPSC 30d.
