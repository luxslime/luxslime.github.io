ClinicalTrials.gov sponsor pipeline monitor vs ParseForge scraper

ClinicalTrials.gov Sponsor Pipeline Scraper (taroyamada/clinical-trials-pipeline-monitor) is the change-row alternative to parseforge/clinicaltrials-scraper when the job is watching new or changed studies on a sponsor/condition watchlist. ParseForge’s Store card is a bulk ClinicalTrials.gov scrape at $8.00 / 1,000 results. Ours publishes $10.00 / 1,000 clinical trial change rows with no API key or login.

Public Store facts side by side

Both Actors read ClinicalTrials.gov without an API key. The billed unit differs: bulk result rows versus delivered new or changed study rows.

When is the pipeline monitor the better fit?

Choose the pipeline monitor when you need recurring digests of new or changed studies against a watchlist and will pay per change row. Choose ParseForge when you need a one-shot bulk pull of trial records by filters and the $8.00 / 1,000 results card is the better fit.
