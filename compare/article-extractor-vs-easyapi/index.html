When is the RAG-oriented article extractor the better fit?

Choose taroyamada/article-content-extractor when the URLs are news, blog, newsroom, or press pages and you want Markdown (default) or text rows for RAG/LLM pipelines, with a documented first paid path around $0.00805. Use broader site/docs extraction on Website Content Extractor instead when pages are not article-shaped. See also article vs website content extractor.
