EUDR Records Manager vs EUDR Guard (App Store compare)

EUDR Records Manager (apps.shopify.com/eudr-records-manager) is the lot-registry alternative to EUDR Guard when you need supplier and commodity-lot records with DDS reference numbers, hashed document packs, and an audit trail. EUDR Guard’s listing centers on finding products in EUDR scope and a supplier due-diligence checklist. Both listings showed a Free plan on 2026-10-30.

When is EUDR Records Manager the better fit?

Choose EUDR Records Manager when you already know scope and need a living registry of supplier lots, DDS refs, hashed packs, and an audit trail. Choose EUDR Guard when the first job is flagging in-scope products and walking a supplier due-diligence checklist.
