AddressFence vs Mighty PO Box Blocker (App Store compare)

PO Box Blocker — AddressFence (apps.shopify.com/address-guard) is a Free-plan alternative to Mighty PO Box Blocker for stopping PO box shipping addresses at Shopify checkout. AddressFence’s listing includes a Free plan that blocks PO boxes and parcel lockers on shipping, plus ZIP rules. Mighty’s listing showed a paid Professional Plan (¥1,555 JPY/month on 2026-10-30) for shipping PO box blocking.

When is AddressFence the better fit?

Choose AddressFence when you want a Free plan that blocks shipping PO boxes and parcel lockers with ZIP rules, and you may later need street-number checks, regions/CSV, or billing-address rules on a paid plan. Choose Mighty when you only need shipping PO box blocking with customized warning messages and accept its paid Professional Plan.
