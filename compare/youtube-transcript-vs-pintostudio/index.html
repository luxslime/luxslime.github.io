YouTube Transcript Scraper vs pintostudio (Store price compare)

YouTube Transcript Scraper (taroyamada/youtube-transcript-bulk-api) is a lower published result PPE alternative to pintostudio/youtube-transcript-scraper for public captions: $2.50 / 1,000 results versus $10.00 / 1,000 results on the Store cards read on 2026-10-30. Both list SRT/VTT-style transcript export. Ours also publishes Actor Start at $0.00005 and states that blocked videos are not charged.

When is the $2.50 / 1k YouTube transcript Actor the better fit?

Choose taroyamada/youtube-transcript-bulk-api when the job is bulk public captions for pipelines, RAG ingestion, or subtitle export, and the published Store card price of $2.50 / 1,000 results matters. The listing documents video, playlist, and channel sources, timed segments, SRT/VTT/text, and that age-restricted, private, deleted, or otherwise blocked videos are not charged as results.

Documented first paid math on this site

This site’s YouTube Transcript page documents a one-video first paid path: if the run charges one Actor Start event and writes one result, PPE is $0.00005 + $0.0025 = $0.00255. That figure is only for the taroyamada listing’s published events.
