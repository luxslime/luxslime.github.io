Different jobs, not a price clone

The episode-scraper listing is built around episode archives and audio file URLs. Apple Podcasts / iTunes Scraper is built around directory discovery and recurring show checks:

  • Official Apple/iTunes endpoints — searchTerm uses the iTunes Search API; lookupIds uses the iTunes Lookup API; chartRequests snapshot Apple rankings.
  • No proxy — public Apple and RSS endpoints; no account session or browser automation.
  • Search + charts + watchlist — one Actor for keyword discovery, chart snapshots, and collection-ID refresh. It does not emit rank-movement reports or review rows; those are separate Actors.

Use the episode scraper when the deliverable is episode-level audio URLs or a full RSS archive. Use this iTunes scraper when the deliverable is matching shows, a chart snapshot, or a watchlist of collectionId values. For a known show’s complete published catalog, a public RSS feed remains the direct source.

How do the jobs differ?

The logiover listing is an episode-oriented scraper: episodes, audio URLs, and RSS archives. Apple Podcasts / iTunes Scraper is search, chart snapshots, and collection-ID watchlists on official Apple/iTunes endpoints, with no proxy required.

When is the iTunes scraper the better fit?

When the job is Apple directory discovery (searchTerm), official chart snapshots (chartRequests), or a watchlist of known collection IDs (lookupIds).
