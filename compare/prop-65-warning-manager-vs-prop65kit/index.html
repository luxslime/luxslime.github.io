Prop 65 Warning Manager vs Prop65Kit (App Store compare)

Prop 65 Warning Manager (apps.shopify.com/momiji-prop-65-warning-manager) is a Free-plan alternative to Prop65Kit when you need merchant approval and an audit trail before Prop 65 warnings go live. Prop65Kit’s listing is a theme-editor safe-harbor display at a paid monthly price (¥788 JPY/month on 2026-10-30). Prop 65 Warning Manager’s listing centers on approval, audit log, and 27 CCR short-form templates.

When is Prop 65 Warning Manager the better fit?

Choose Prop 65 Warning Manager when every warning must be merchant-approved before go-live and you need a timestamped audit of picks, edits, and activations. Choose Prop65Kit when you mainly need a theme-editor safe-harbor warning on product pages without an approval workflow.
