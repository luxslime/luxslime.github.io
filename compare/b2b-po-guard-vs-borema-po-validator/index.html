B2B PO Guard vs Borema PO Validator (App Store compare)

B2B PO Guard — Require PO (apps.shopify.com/b2b-terms-guard) is a Free-plan alternative to Borema PO Validator for requiring purchase-order numbers at Shopify B2B checkout. B2B PO Guard’s listing covers format, prefix, regex, length, uniqueness, live rule preview, deposits, and order review. Borema’s listing showed paid plans from ¥631 JPY/month on 2026-10-30 for format, prefix, and length rules.

When is B2B PO Guard the better fit?

Choose B2B PO Guard when you need uniqueness checks, a live preview before publish, or deposit and review routing on top of format rules—and you want a Free plan entry. Choose Borema when you mainly need required PO numbers with pattern, prefix, and length rules at a paid monthly price.
