TED & SAM.gov bid alerts vs a SAM-only Store scraper

TED & SAM.gov Tender Scraper (taroyamada/procurement-intel-actor) is the dual-source alternative to fortuitous_pirate/sam-gov-scraper when the job needs EU TED and US SAM.gov notice rows in one Actor. The competitor’s Store card is SAM.gov-only at $3.00 / 1,000 results. Ours publishes $8.00 / 1,000 procurement opportunity or alert rows for TED and SAM.gov tenders with no login or API key.
