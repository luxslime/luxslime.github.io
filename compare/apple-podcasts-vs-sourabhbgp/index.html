Different jobs, not a clone

The sourabhbgp listing’s Store description centers on reviews, charts, episode archives, publisher catalogs, and ten modes across storefronts. Apple Podcasts Scraper on this site is built for keyword search (searchTerm), chart snapshots (chartRequests), and collection-ID watchlists (lookupIds) on official Apple/iTunes endpoints, with optional RSS recent episodes. It does not claim review-text scraping as the primary job on the listing page.
