How is SEC EDGAR Scraper different from SEC & ESG Report Scraper and SEC Material Contract Intelligence?

SEC EDGAR Scraper (taroyamada/sec-edgar-filings-monitor): This Actor lists recent SEC EDGAR filings for companies you supply (ticker/CIK), filtered by formTypes and daysBack, and emits source-linked filing rows. Default example watches 8-K and S-1. Not investment advice. Not an ESG report pack (use SEC & ESG Report Scraper). Not Form 4 insider trades or exhibit-contract extraction. SEC & ESG Report Scraper is for ESG/climate packs (input company disclosure targets; SEC EDGAR + reports; disclosure change rows). Not 8-K/S-1 filing alerts alone. SEC Material Contract Intelligence is for SEC exhibit contracts (input tickers / CIKs; SEC EDGAR exhibits; contract-intel rows). Not Form 4 insider trades. SEC Insider Transaction Scraper is for Forms 3/4/5 trades (input issuers; SEC EDGAR ownership; insider-trade rows). Not 8-K material contracts.
