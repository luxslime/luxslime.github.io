Product JSON-LD field checker

Direct answer. Paste the Product JSON-LD from a product page (the contents of its application/ld+json script) or lines such as title: Merino Wool Beanie and gtin: 4006381333931. The page shows which fields that Google’s merchant listing documentation and Merchant Center product data specification list as required or recommended are present or missing, each with a question to check. It does not decide eligibility for any search feature or AI agent. It runs in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.

This is a reading aid, not what any app checks. Where the checks come from

The rows follow Google Search Central’s merchant listing structured data documentation (Product: name, image and offers required; description, brand.name, gtin, mpn and sku recommended; Offer: price and priceCurrency required, price above zero for merchant listings; availability, itemCondition, shippingDetails and hasMerchantReturnPolicy recommended, with store-wide shipping and return policies suggested under Organization markup), Google Merchant Center’s product data specification (title up to 150 characters, brand for new products, valid GTIN check digits, MPN when there is no GTIN, no “N/A” or “Generic” brands) and the schema.org Product and Offer types.

The page reads one Product (or the first variant of a ProductGroup) from what you paste. It cannot see your live page, your feed settings or what Google or any AI agent actually does with the data, and requirements differ by country and product category.
