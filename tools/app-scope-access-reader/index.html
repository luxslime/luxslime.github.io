How the groups work

  • Customer or order data: scopes for customers, orders and draft orders, read or write.
  • Store-wide change or finance: scopes that can edit themes, scripts, checkout branding or content, or that reach payments or staff data.
  • Other write: any other write_ scope, which lets an app change data and not only read it.
  • Read-only: read_ and unauthenticated_ scopes outside the groups above.

The groups come from the scope names alone. They are a reading aid for a review conversation, not a risk score: the same scope can be reasonable for one app and unnecessary for another. Where to find the list: the permissions screen shown when an app is installed, or the app’s own listing or support page.
