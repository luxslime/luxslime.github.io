Shopify challenges

Prop 65 short-form warnings need a chemical name on 2028 product pages

Prop 65 warning templates citing 27 CCR on Shopify

Prop 65 on Shopify: California-only is a jurisdiction job, not an IP pop-up

Ship-to-California is a checkout job — US-CA on the disclosure is not the same control

Alcoholic beverages use the Prop 65 alcohol disclosure type — not the chemical cancer / reproductive types

A Prop 65 product-page disclosure does not automatically appear on the cart — cart is a separate display surface

Prop 65 food short-form on Shopify: /food and no triangle

Block PO Box on the billing address, not just shipping

Built-in Shopify checkout address rules cannot target billing

Test a Shopify address rule in a simulator before you publish

Requiring shipping and billing to match does not detect PO Boxes — it only forces the two addresses to be identical

Generic environmental claims on product copy are banned in the EU from 27 September 2026

Compostable and biodegradable claims on Shopify product copy need a cited scan — not a freehand “eco” synonym

Recycled-content % claims on Shopify product copy

EmpCo recyclable claim wording on Shopify product copy

Dated evidence records for Shopify green claims

Flag green-claim wording and see which provision it cites

Vague Umweltaussagen on a DE-facing product page need same-medium clarity — UWG EmpCo rules from 27 September 2026

Unqualified “Made in USA” on a product page needs all-or-virtually-all U.S. content — or a clear qualified claim

“Carbon neutral” on the product page: EmpCo bans offset-based neutrality — not the same job as “eco-friendly”

Supplier lots need an EUDR DDS reference number on file before 30 December 2026

A DDS lot registry is not a weekly gap review — missing supplier, country, or DDS fields still slip

An EUDR export folder is not a SHA-256 hashed document pack tied to source records

Staff permission drift after the last review is not caught by a one-time user list

B2B checkout can skip a purchase-order number unless a validation rule requires it

Prevent duplicate PO numbers on Shopify B2B checkout

Requiring a PO number is not the same as checking format, prefix, regex, or length

B2B PO rules should no-op on D2C checkout

Preview a Shopify B2B PO rule before you publish

A deposit % is not Net Terms and not a required PO number — three different checkout jobs

Native Net Terms set when payment is due — PO and deposit rules are separate checkout jobs

Holding oversized B2B orders for approval is not a required PO and not Net Terms

AI shopping agents skip catalogs without crawler rules and complete product JSON-LD

A theme that emits some Product JSON-LD is not a catalog-wide JSON-LD and Open Graph completeness audit

AI catalog readiness drifts — schedule scans and get alerts when the score changes

A readiness score is not enough — review fix guidance, then rerun the scan

AI catalog readiness includes policy, shipping, tax, and domain — not only robots.txt and Product JSON-LD

Shopify already serves /agents.md — customize it with agents.md.liquid, not an app proxy

If robots.txt disallows AI crawlers, agents.md cannot save AI shopping visibility

GTIN is an AI-catalog input — put the GS1 barcode first when you store more than one

Open Graph is not Product JSON-LD — both can be incomplete on a live Shopify PDP

The Search Console warning is non-critical — set returns and shipping once (Search Console, Merchant Center, or Organization markup) before hand-editing every offer

CLP hazard pictograms belong on the product page, not only on the parcel

A theme paste is not a CLP hazard profile — build GHS pictograms and statements first

CLP hazard on the product page needs synced metafields — not a one-off theme paste

UFI on the product page: part of the Art. 17 offer block from 1 Jan 2028 — not a substitute for the pack label

Review missing CLP hazard fields before you publish

An SDS download link is not CLP hazard display on the product page

EU textile fibre composition must use Annex I names, not trade names

Annex I fibre names are not environmental-impact fields — record impact information separately

Textile EPR registration is a retained record job — not the same duty as Annex I fibre names

Fibre composition must be in the official language(s) of the Member State — Annex I names alone are not enough

“Bamboo” is not an EU fibre name — the composition line on the product page says viscose (or the Annex I name that matches the process)

France places of manufacture vs EU fibre names (Annex I): two separate textile duties on Shopify

France: show places of manufacture next to the price on Shopify (Code de l'environnement L. 541-9-1-2)

Food allergen labelling has to appear before purchase, not only on delivery

An allergen in the ingredient text that never appears on the declared list is a FIC gap

A one-off allergen check is not a weekly FIC completeness review with an audit trail

Storage conditions are a before-purchase FIC gap — not only allergens and nutrition

EU distance selling: nutrition declaration and net quantity belong on the product page, not only the parcel

Distance-sale food pages need the food business operator’s name and address before purchase — not a GPSR RP field

The best-before or use-by date can wait until delivery — every other mandatory particular cannot

EU honey origin: list countries (and blend %) on the product page before purchase — not only on the jar

Cyber Resilience Act Article 14 duties need a product file before an incident

Encrypted ENISA SRP draft packs for CRA Article 14

A CRA duty file is not done until open record gaps are closed — role checklists show what is missing

CRA duty work needs exportable record packs and a chronological audit trail — not a scattered file folder

Article 14 clocks are not the same job as an ENISA draft pack or an open-gap checklist

Show the CRA support-period end date on the Shopify product page at purchase — the duty file is not the offer

Importers and distributors forward vulnerabilities to the manufacturer — Article 14 ENISA filing is the manufacturer’s job

EU right-to-repair proof needs revision-linked forms, not a loose email thread

Verify the purchase before you accept a repair request — forms come after intake

A repair request is five stages, not one email thread — record diagnosis and quote before work starts, and close the job with the return

A repair job needs a redacted record trail — not an unredacted mailbox thread

EmpCo on the product page: show the repairability score when it exists — otherwise producer-supplied spare-parts facts, not invented copy

Leftover Admin access: leavers with 2FA, collaborators vs the bill, preview vs publish

Cannot suspend a Shopify collaborator — use Remove collaborator account, not staff Suspend

90-day collaborator expiry is not Suspend — use Reactivate (or Remove + a new request)

30/90-day access-review reminders are not Shopify’s 90-day collaborator expiry

Shopify app ownership evidence: scopes and owner

Audit Shopify app scopes for customer data access

A scope list is not a risk score — rate each install Low, Moderate, Elevated, or High

Shopify app scope, owner, and cost drift alerts

Collaborator request code: only Partners you share the code with can ask for access

Block PO boxes: native shipping-rate hide vs checkout validation vs AddressFence

Require a street number at Shopify checkout after the Address Blocker sunset

Prop 65: native product disclosures vs an approval and audit-log app

Prop 65 storefront activation audit on Shopify

Every Prop 65 warning needs merchant approval before go-live — not theme-only paste

Prop 65 warning assignment across the Shopify catalog

EUDR: who is in scope on 30 December 2026 vs mid-2027, and what records to keep

Stop retyping GPSR manufacturer and responsible-person fields on every product
