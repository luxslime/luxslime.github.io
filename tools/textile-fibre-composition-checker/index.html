Textile fibre composition checker

Direct answer. Paste one fibre per line with its percentage, for example 60% cotton. The page shows whether each name is on the English list in Annex I of Regulation (EU) No 1007/2011, points out trade names such as Lycra or Tencel with the Annex I name to consider, and adds up your percentages. It runs in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.

This is a quick self-check, not legal advice and not what any app checks. To check every fibre in your catalog against the Annex I catalogue in English, German and French, try FibreLedger Textile Labels (Shopify App Store).

What the checker looks at

  • Fibre names: each line is compared with the English names in Annex I of Regulation (EU) No 1007/2011 on textile fibre names and labelling, including the animal-hair and plant-fibre names.
  • Trade and common names: a short list of names such as Lycra, Tencel, Spandex or rayon is shown with the Annex I name to consider. See the guide on trade names like Lycra and Tencel and on bamboo and viscose.
  • Percentages: the numbers you give are added up and compared with 100%.

Write one fibre per line, for example 60% cotton. Names in other languages, the “other fibres” wording, tolerances and the rules for linings and decorative parts are not checked. A line marked “Annex I name” only means the name is on the list. Confirm against the regulation text and read the EU textile label guide.
