Key dates in Regulation (EU) 2024/2847

Article 71 sets three application dates. They come from the regulation text, not from this page’s matching.

  • 11 June 2026: Chapter IV (Articles 35 to 51, notification of conformity assessment bodies) applies.
  • 11 September 2026: Article 14, the reporting duties of manufacturers, applies. Under Article 69(3) it applies to all in-scope products, including those placed on the market before 11 December 2027.
  • 11 December 2027: the rest of the Regulation applies. Under Article 69(2), products placed on the market before that date are subject to it only if they undergo a substantial modification from that date.

Where the questions come from

Roles come from Article 3 (manufacturer in point 13, importer in 16, distributor in 17) and Articles 19 to 22. Scope comes from Article 2 and the definitions in Article 3. The Annex III and Annex IV prompts name categories listed in those annexes. The page matches words in what you type; it cannot judge intended purpose, substantial modification or whether another EU act applies. Read the regulation text on EUR-Lex and the guides on CRA duty records, role checklists and Article 14 deadlines.
