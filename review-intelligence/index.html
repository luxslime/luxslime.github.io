Apple/iTunes podcast reviews

Apple Podcasts Reviews Scraper reads the official Apple/iTunes Customer Reviews RSS JSON for collection IDs and storefronts you pass. It is not a keyword search, episode catalog, or Top Podcasts rank tool. At most 25 unique iTunes collection IDs, 10 unique ISO-3166-1 alpha-2 country codes, and 10 review pages per show/country pair are accepted. Over-limit input fails instead of being silently truncated.

{
  "podcastIds": ["1535809341"],
  "countries": ["us"],
  "maxPagesPerPair": 1,
  "generateReport": false,
  "maxChargeUsd": 0.5,
  "delivery": "dataset",
  "dryRun": false
}

Published raw fields include podcastId, country, page, reviewerName, rating, title, content, version, voteCount, updated, and reviewId, plus sourceUrl (the official iTunes Customer Reviews RSS URL). Set generateReport to true for one podcast_review_report. Set mode to monitor with a stable monitorKey to emit only new or changed reviews after a baseline_only first run.

Shopify App Store merchant listings

Shopify App Store Review Scraper fetches apps.shopify.com/{slug} listing and paginated review pages. appUrls is required (full listing URLs or bare slugs). Optional published fields are reviewLimit (default 100, minimum 1, maximum 500), timeoutMs, delivery, webhookUrl, and dryRun. The published input schema does not include appSlugs, maxReviewsPerApp, sinceDays, or sortBy.

{
  "appUrls": ["https://apps.shopify.com/omnisend"],
  "reviewLimit": 100,
  "timeoutMs": 20000,
  "delivery": "dataset",
  "dryRun": false
}

A good first run gives you apps[] (rating, pricing hints, AI summary text, recurring themes), reviewSamples[] plus rollups, and warnings / endpointStatuses when review pages are thin or a listing surface degrades.

What is the Review Intelligence pack?

This hub is a chooser for the two public-review landings already on this site: Apple Podcasts Reviews Scraper and Shopify App Store Review Scraper. They read different sources, take different inputs, and emit different row shapes. Use this page to pick the matching landing; then open that Actor on the Apify Store from the confirmed Store URL on that landing.

How do I choose between Apple Podcasts Reviews Scraper and Shopify App Store Review Scraper?

Use Apple Podcasts Reviews Scraper when the source is public Apple/iTunes Customer Reviews RSS JSON for podcast collection IDs and storefronts you already have. Use Shopify App Store Review Scraper when the source is a public apps.shopify.com listing (URL or slug) and you need merchant ratings, pricing hints, sampled reviews, and theme rollups. Podcast show ratings do not belong on the Shopify Actor. Shopify merchant listings do not belong on the Apple reviews Actor.

Can one Actor scrape both podcast and Shopify App Store reviews?

No. Apple Podcasts Reviews Scraper does not scrape Shopify apps. Shopify App Store Review Scraper does not accept podcast IDs, Apple show URLs, or episode feeds. Run the Actor whose source matches the catalog you need.

What does each Actor deliver?

Apple Podcasts Reviews Scraper delivers podcast_review rows (rating, title, content, storefront, sourceUrl) or one podcast_review_report when generateReport is true, and can emit only new or changed reviews in monitor mode. Shopify App Store Review Scraper delivers one app-level report per listing: listing metadata, rating summary, pricing hints, Shopify listing AI review summary, sampled merchant reviews, and keyword theme rollups.
