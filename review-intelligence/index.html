Apple/iTunes podcast reviews

Apple Podcasts Reviews Scraper reads the official Apple/iTunes Customer Reviews RSS JSON for collection IDs and storefronts you pass. It is not a keyword search, episode catalog, or Top Podcasts rank tool. At most 25 unique iTunes collection IDs, 10 unique ISO-3166-1 alpha-2 country codes, and 10 review pages per show/country pair are accepted. Over-limit input fails instead of being silently truncated.

{
  "podcastIds": ["1535809341"],
  "countries": ["us"],
  "maxPagesPerPair": 1,
  "generateReport": false,
  "maxChargeUsd": 0.5,
  "delivery": "dataset",
  "dryRun": false
}

Published raw fields include podcastId, country, page, reviewerName, rating, title, content, version, voteCount, updated, and reviewId, plus sourceUrl (the official iTunes Customer Reviews RSS URL). Set generateReport to true for one podcast_review_report. Set mode to monitor with a stable monitorKey to emit only new or changed reviews after a baseline_only first run.

Shopify App Store merchant listings

Shopify App Store Review Scraper fetches apps.shopify.com/{slug} listing and paginated review pages. appUrls is required (full listing URLs or bare slugs). Optional published fields are reviewLimit (default 100, minimum 1, maximum 500), timeoutMs, delivery, webhookUrl, and dryRun. The published input schema does not include appSlugs, maxReviewsPerApp, sinceDays, or sortBy.

{
  "appUrls": ["https://apps.shopify.com/omnisend"],
  "reviewLimit": 100,
  "timeoutMs": 20000,
  "delivery": "dataset",
  "dryRun": false
}

A good first run gives you apps[] (rating, pricing hints, AI summary text, recurring themes), reviewSamples[] plus rollups, and warnings / endpointStatuses when review pages are thin or a listing surface degrades.

G2 and Capterra B2B software reviews

G2 & Capterra Review Scraper reads public G2 and Capterra product review pages. Required input is reviewPageUrls in the published formats g2.com/products/{slug}/reviews and capterra.com/p/{id}/{slug}/reviews/. It auto-detects source and normalizes product metadata, ratings, and review rows with pros and cons. Optional published fields are reviewLimit (default 200), delivery, webhookUrl, and dryRun. The published input schema does not include products, source, slug, maxReviewsPerProduct, or emitDeltas.

{
  "reviewPageUrls": [
    "https://www.g2.com/products/notion/reviews",
    "https://www.capterra.com/p/147384/Notion/reviews/"
  ],
  "reviewLimit": 200,
  "delivery": "dataset",
  "dryRun": false
}

A good first run gives you a per-product row with ratings and review samples, plus status / warnings when a page is thin, blocked, or structurally changed. dryRun true validates input and returns scaffold output without fetching live data.

What is the Review Intelligence pack?

This hub is a chooser for the three public-review landings already on this site: Apple Podcasts Reviews Scraper, Shopify App Store Review Scraper, and G2 & Capterra Review Scraper. They read different sources, take different inputs, and emit different row shapes. Use this page to pick the matching landing; then open that Actor on the Apify Store from the confirmed Store URL on that landing.

How do I choose among Apple Podcasts, Shopify App Store, and G2/Capterra review scrapers?

Use Apple Podcasts Reviews Scraper when the source is public Apple/iTunes Customer Reviews RSS JSON for podcast collection IDs and storefronts you already have. Use Shopify App Store Review Scraper when the source is a public apps.shopify.com listing (URL or slug) and you need merchant ratings, pricing hints, sampled reviews, and theme rollups. Use G2 & Capterra Review Scraper when the source is a public G2 or Capterra product review page (reviewPageUrls) and you need normalized B2B ratings, rating breakdowns, and review rows with pros and cons.

Can one Actor scrape podcast, Shopify, and G2/Capterra reviews together?

No. Apple Podcasts Reviews Scraper does not scrape Shopify apps or G2/Capterra pages. Shopify App Store Review Scraper does not accept podcast IDs, Apple show URLs, episode feeds, or G2/Capterra review URLs. G2 & Capterra Review Scraper does not accept Shopify slugs, podcast IDs, Apple show URLs, or episode feeds. Run the Actor whose source matches the catalog you need.

What does each Actor deliver?

Apple Podcasts Reviews Scraper delivers podcast_review rows (rating, title, content, storefront, sourceUrl) or one podcast_review_report when generateReport is true, and can emit only new or changed reviews in monitor mode. Shopify App Store Review Scraper delivers one app-level report per listing: listing metadata, rating summary, pricing hints, Shopify listing AI review summary, sampled merchant reviews, and keyword theme rollups. G2 & Capterra Review Scraper delivers a per-product row with source (g2 or capterra), productName, overallRating, totalReviewCount, ratingBreakdown, reviews[] (title, rating, date, author, body, pros, cons, verified), and status.
