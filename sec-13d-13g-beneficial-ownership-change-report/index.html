How is SEC 13D & 13G Beneficial Ownership Change Report different from SEC EDGAR Scraper and SEC Insider Transaction Scraper?

SEC 13D & 13G Beneficial Ownership Change Report (taroyamada/sec-13d-13g-beneficial-ownership-change-report): Track issuer watchlists using official SEC EDGAR Schedule 13D, 13D/A, 13G, and 13G/A filings. Receive source-linked beneficial ownership change alerts, reports, and optional exports. Not Form 4 insider trades. Not 13F institutional tables. Not a general 8-K monitor. Live schema requires watchlists and userAgent. SEC EDGAR Scraper is for 8-K and S-1 filing alerts (input tickers / CIKs; EDGAR; Filing rows). Not Schedule 13D/13G. SEC Insider Transaction Scraper is for Form 4 insider trades (input tickers / CIKs; EDGAR Form 4; Transaction rows). Not 13D/13G beneficial ownership. SEC 13F Institutional Holdings Change Report is for 13F holdings tables (input watchlists + userAgent; EDGAR 13F; Holdings-change alerts). Not 13D/13G schedules.
