How is CPSC Product Recall Marketplace Watch different from Product Safety Recall Scraper and NHTSA Recall Scraper?

CPSC Product Recall Marketplace Watch (taroyamada/cpsc-product-recall-marketplace-watch-scraper): This Actor tracks official CPSC recall records by product, brand, category, and marketplace watch terms with source-linked alert and report rows. Not FDA warning letters. Not NHTSA vehicle recalls. Not a marketplace listing scraper. Informational only. Not legal or safety advice. Product Safety Recall Scraper is for FDA/CPSC recall digests (input feeds; FDA/CPSC recall feeds; Recall digest rows). Not a CPSC marketplace watch with alert/report PPE. NHTSA Recall Scraper is for NHTSA recall digests (input vehicle watches; NHTSA public APIs; Recall digest rows). Not the alert/digest/report watch Actor. FDA Warning Letters Scraper is for FDA warning-letter digests (input feeds; FDA warning-letter feeds; Warning-letter digest rows). Not CPSC/NHTSA/EPA records.
