How is Weather Forecast Scraper different from Forex Exchange Rate Scraper and RSS & Atom Feed Extractor?

Weather Forecast Scraper (taroyamada/weather-forecast-fetcher): This Actor calls the Open-Meteo API for locations you supply (name and/or lat/lon per live schema) and emits hourly/daily forecast rows. Public weather API only. Not a meteorological warning service. Not RSS weather feeds. Not financial or operational advice. Forex Exchange Rate Scraper is for FX snapshots (input baseCurrency; open.er-api.com; exchange-rate snapshots). Not Treasury fiscal series. RSS & Atom Feed Extractor is for Known feed XML (input feed URLs; RSS/Atom; feed items). Not Open-Meteo forecasts. US Treasury Data Scraper is for Treasury fiscal series (input feeds; Treasury Fiscal Data API; fiscal change rows). Not FX mid-market rates.

How do dataset, webhook, and dry-run delivery work?

Dataset output is the billable surface when rows are written. webhookUrl is used when delivery is webhook (and typically not during dryRun). dryRun true validates or samples without the usual dataset/webhook side effects described on the Store schema.
