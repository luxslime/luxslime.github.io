Extract headline, byline, and article body from news, blog, and press URLs

Article Content Extractor fetches public news, blog, newsroom, and press pages you already have and returns structured article rows: headline, byline, publish date, body, excerpt, and hero image. It is a bounded HTML cleanup step for research, content QA, and RAG ingestion — not a ranking tool, a full-site crawler, or a Google News search. For docs, pricing, policy, product, and help-center pages, use Website Content Extractor. For query-based headline discovery, use Google News Scraper.

  • Source extraction for content QA (title, metadata, thin-content, and page-quality checks).

  • Optionally request a source-linked content-audit report or batch export when at least one article is usable.

Low-cost article extraction

How is Article Content Extractor different from Website Content Extractor?

This Actor parses public news, blog, newsroom, and press pages into article rows: headline, author, publishedAt, articleText, excerpt, and heroImage. Maximum 300 URLs per run. outputFormat is markdown (default) or text — it does not emit HTML. Website Content Extractor cleans docs, product, pricing, policy, and help-center pages that are not article-shaped (maximum 200 URLs) and can emit markdown, text, or HTML. Route broad site pages there.

How is Article Content Extractor different from Google News Scraper and RSS discovery?

This Actor is HTML cleanup for article-shaped URLs you already have. It is not a search surface. Google News Scraper is query-based Google News RSS discovery (queries, maximum 50) and returns headlines and article URLs without full article text. RSS & Atom Feed Extractor parses known-publisher feed XML (feedUrls, maximum 50) and does not crawl page HTML. Discover URLs first, then send news, blog, and press links here.

Which public article URLs can I pass, and what is rejected?

Pass public HTTP(S) news, blog, newsroom, or press URLs you own or are authorized to fetch. Maximum 300 URLs per run. Loopback, private, link-local, metadata, reserved, and DNS-resolved private targets are rejected, as are non-HTML responses, excessive redirects, and oversized responses. Default bounds are 3 redirects and 5 MiB per HTML response; the runtime never permits more than 10 redirects or 10 MiB. Schema prefill is three blog.google article URLs.

What fields does a useful article row include?

The published README field list is rowType, url, headline, author, publishedAt, articleText, excerpt, heroImage, and sourceUrl. Sample rowType is article. Rows also include fetch timestamps, warnings when a source is partial or changed, and stable IDs when the workflow supports recurring change detection. Optional inline images when includeImages is true; heroImage can still be returned separately. The README sample is placeholder article data, not a live coverage guarantee. There is no separate output schema on the Store page.

What published input starts an Article Content Extractor run?

urls is required. The published schema has no maxChargeUsd and no includeMetadata. Live input fields:

Field Type Default Notes
urls string[] required Public article, news, blog, newsroom, or press URLs (max 300). Schema prefill is three blog.google article URLs.
outputFormat string markdown Enum: text or markdown. No html.
includeImages boolean true Include the inline images array. heroImage can still be returned separately. Store Quickstart uses false.
concurrency integer 5 Parallel article requests (1–10).
timeoutMs integer 15000 Request timeout per article in milliseconds.
maxResponseBytes integer 5242880 Reject HTML responses larger than this limit before parsing (5 MiB default; runtime cap 10 MiB).
maxRedirects integer 3 Maximum public HTTP redirects followed per article (runtime cap 10).
delivery string dataset Enum: dataset or webhook.
webhookUrl string — POST target when delivery is webhook. Skipped on dryRun.
generateReport boolean false Emit one article-content-audit-report when at least one article is usable.
emitExport boolean false Emit one article-batch-export when at least one article is usable.
dryRun boolean false Validate input and write only local output. No dataset, webhook, or payable rows.

Do not send unpublished aliases such as includeMetadata, maxChargeUsd, html as outputFormat, Google News queries, RSS feedUrls, authorizedUseConfirmed, or emitUnchanged. Those belong on other Actors.

How does Article Content Extractor deliver dataset, webhook, and dry-run output?

delivery defaults to dataset. A non-dry-run always writes canonical dataset rows first; webhook delivery runs only after dataset and pay-per-event output succeed. A webhook is sent only after dataset billing succeeds and is best effort; a failed webhook is not retried by the Actor and never creates a second charge. webhookUrl is used when delivery is webhook and is skipped on dryRun. dryRun validates input and writes only local output — no dataset writes, webhooks, or payable rows.

Does Article Content Extractor emit HTML output?

No. The published outputFormat enum is markdown (default) or text. This Actor does not emit HTML. Website Content Extractor is the sibling that can return html as well as markdown or text.

Does includeImages replace heroImage or add an inline images array?

includeImages defaults to true and includes the inline images array in the result. The heroImage field can still be returned separately when available. The Store Quickstart sets includeImages false for a low-cost first run. heroImage is in the published README field list either way.

Does Article Content Extractor crawl a full site or rank pages?

No. You supply the URL list. Maximum 300 URLs per run. It is a bounded source extractor for research, content QA, and RAG ingestion — not a ranking tool, a full-site crawler, or a claim of publisher endorsement. It does not imply content ownership transfer. Only fetch public HTTP(S) URLs that you own or are authorized to audit.

Are failed or empty article fetches billed as useful article rows?

No. Failed or no-content rows stay out of the default dataset. The billed useful-article-row event is for one successfully extracted or partially extracted public article row ($0.008). Dry runs, validation-only runs, error-only runs, and no-usable-row recurring polls do not write payable rows. The default dataset is the canonical billable surface.

Does Article Content Extractor accept maxChargeUsd or includeMetadata?

No. Those are Website Content Extractor fields. This Actor’s published input has no maxChargeUsd safety cap and no includeMetadata flag. Do not send html as outputFormat, Google News queries, RSS feedUrls, or Site QA-only fields such as authorizedUseConfirmed and emitUnchanged.

Can I send Google News queries or RSS feed URLs into Article Content Extractor?

No. Required input is a urls list of article-shaped HTTP(S) pages. For query-based Google News RSS discovery use Google News Scraper (queries). For known-publisher feed XML use RSS & Atom Feed Extractor (feedUrls). After those discovery runs, send news, blog, and press page URLs here and docs, pricing, policy, or product URLs to Website Content Extractor.
